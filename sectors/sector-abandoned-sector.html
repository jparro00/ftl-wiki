Sector profile · LANIUS_SECTOR

Abandoned Sector

The only sector outside the nebulas that reserves beacons for weather: HOSTILE_ENVIRONMENT_LANIUS drops one or two of your stops into an asteroid field, beside a pulsar, or under a planetary Anti-Ship Battery — and that last one is firing at the Lanius, not at you. Everything else is negotiation. The Lanius want metal, not your ship, so most of what reads as neutral is a trade that a bad roll turns into a fight, and the prize for talking instead of shooting is the crew nobody else stocks: a Lanius drains the air out of the room it stands in, which is free labour behind good doors and a slow leak everywhere else.

Solid blocks are the minimum the sector must place; faded blocks are the optional extra.

Lines are filled in this order, and the map holds at most 24 beacons (19–24 by the community wiki's count). When they run out, generation stops — a line near the bottom can receive nothing at all.

This sector allocates up to 27 slots against 24 beacons, so the bottom of the table is a wish list rather than a promise.

Beacons still empty at the end of the table are filled from the shared NEUTRAL list (OVERRIDE_NEUTRAL under Advanced Edition).

The exit beacon is not in this table at all — it draws from the shared EXIT_LIST, and an exit inside a cloud is always empty.

Entry beacon is always START_BEACON_LANIUS.

Nothing is guaranteed here except the fighting. Stores are pinned at exactly two with no variance, the quest entry is allocated 0–1 so a run can cross the sector and never see one, and no ship unlocks here. The overrun is mild and lands in exactly one place: everything above the neutral line fits the map even at its maximum, so a crowded map can only shave the tail of NEUTRAL_LANIUS — nothing here is ever cut outright. Only the Engi sectors place more item beacons than this one.

The list no other sector carries outside a nebula — and it is weather, not oxygen. The asteroid field's rocks arrive faster the more your own shields are upgraded; the pulsar ionises two of your systems every 11–18 seconds and is the one hazard in the game that needs Advanced Edition switched on; and at Lanius fight with friendly ASB support the battery is pointed at the enemy for once, which can repair 8 hull when the shooting stops. The asteroids and the pulsar also hold you at IN DANGER for as long as you stay — no upgrades, no cargo bay, no crew management until you jump out.

Blue options that pay here

  • Lanius ×9Five in the neutral pool, three at the item beacons, one distress. They are the difference between Lanius lone ship opening a store outright and rolling for a fight, and between 45 scrap and 35 for an augment at Lanius craftsmen.
  • Piloting ×2Level 2 auto-pilots the debris at Lanius ship in rich debris field and level 3 does it for a bigger haul, no fight either way; level 2 also strips Lanius powered-down ship without waking the crew inside.
  • Sensors ×3Level 2 or 3 finds the fuel and drone parts left behind on the abandoned platform at Refueling platform garbled broadcast, level 3 also opens Deactivated Auto-ship, and either level salvages the dead-end roll on the Encrypted federation signal chain.
  • Blast Doors ×1Door System 2 turns the Refueling platform garbled broadcast ambush into 5 fuel and no boarders at all.
  • Hull Repair Drone ×1The one branch at Lanius ship absorbing jump beacon that ends with a Lanius joining you instead of scrap leaving you.
  • Everything else ×1 eachCloaking, Medbay, Clone Bay, an ion weapon, Slug, Rockman, Engi, a Crew Teleporter, Long-Ranged Scanners, a Scrap Recovery Arm and a System Repair Drone each appear on exactly one event.

What you can leave with

  • GearLanius craftsmen is a forge: 45 scrap for an augment, 50 for a weapon, 40 for a drone schematic — 35/40/30 with a Lanius helping. It is the sector's only named-item source.
  • AugmentLanius ship absorbing jump beacon trades a random augment for 30 scrap, or — with a Lanius aboard — for 6 missiles or 6 drone parts instead.
  • CrewTwo ways to buy a Lanius outside a store: 40 scrap for the "translation device" at Lanius trader with translator, or the drone option at Lanius ship absorbing jump beacon. No other sector will offer you one, in a store or out of it.
  • ScrapBoth traders swap 3–7 of a resource for 15–50 scrap; the Lanius option at Lanius trader lifts both ends of every band.
  • FuelRefueling platform garbled broadcast is the fuel beacon — 3–5 for taking the station, a flat 5 behind Blast Doors, and 1–3 more on top with Sensors.
  • HullLanius fight with friendly ASB support can hand back 8 hull once the battery finishes the job, and the quest chain can repair 35.

What can bite

  • CrewOnly two events in the whole pool can kill a crew member — Fire on research station and Single life form on moon — and neither of them is a Lanius event. The Lanius take metal, not people.
  • BoardersThree events put someone aboard: 3–4 humans at Boarders: Humans (Abandoned), 2–3 at Encrypted federation signal, and one Lanius at Refueling platform garbled broadcast that vents every room it walks through while you fight it.
  • AmbushNineteen events can end in combat without starting there. Lanius powered-down ship wakes up if you scan it, Lanius ship absorbing jump beacon can chase you as you leave, and Lanius lone ship goes either way on a roll.
  • SystemsDocking at Refueling platform garbled broadcast can cost 3 damage to Engines before the Lanius ship even shows on sensors.
  • Fleet delayThree events can push the fleet back a jump — Lanius ship absorbing rebel base, Lanius ship absorbing automated scout and Pirate briber — but only the first offers it without a fight, and only reliably with a Lanius to explain the idea.

Reading the map here

  • No cloudsThe sector allocates no nebula line at all, so sensors work at every beacon, no cloud can hide a beacon from a quest marker, and nothing on this map slows the fleet by standing still in it. The only brake is the three events that offer one.
  • HazardsLong-Ranged Scanners is the only thing that will name the asteroid field or the pulsar before you commit the jump. Without it, the first warning you get is arriving, and arriving is already too late to move a crew member.
  • DistressRare sector: what the table allocates is exactly what the map shows. No event outside the distress line carries the tag, and none inside it is missing one, so a distress marker here is one of one or two, not a leak from a pool placed earlier.
  • A ship on scannersFour of the twelve distress events read as a possible ship rather than a plain location: the Lanius attacking a civilian, the Lanius trap, the pirate trap, and the friendly out of fuel. Two of those four open in combat.

Generation rules per the community's reverse-engineering of the map generator; the game files state none of them.

minSector in the files is zero-indexed, so the "earliest sector" chip is that value plus one — the number a player counts from the start of a run. The community wiki states the same offset.

The 24-beacon ceiling follows from the 6×4 grid; the 19-beacon floor is stated by the community wiki alone and is not derivable from anything else here, so the at-risk marks use the ceiling.

"Unique" is the files' own unique="true". Whether that means once per sector or once per run is not stated in any file here; the wiki's better bet is once per sector.

The shipped event lists carry no weights, so no beacon here has stated odds. A pool entry listed twice is counted as ×2 and marked.
