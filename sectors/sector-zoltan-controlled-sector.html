Sector profile · ZOLTAN_SECTOR

Zoltan Controlled Sector

One beacon here is fixed: the Zoltan research facility, where a Damaged Stasis Pod becomes a Crystal crew member. Everything else is Zoltan bureaucracy — checkpoints, customs sweeps, docking permits, entry fees — and the fights that come out of it are mostly ones you agreed to. Only two of the seven events on the guaranteed-hostile list are actually Zoltan; the rest are pirates, Mantis, a jumpy Engi escort and the Rebel fleet flying under Zoltan flavour text.

Zoltan crew buys you almost nothing here. Of the 16 gated events in the pool, exactly one blue option asks for a Zoltan — the Zoltan trade hub entry fee, and it still costs 10 scrap. What this sector actually rewards is a Crew Teleporter and Cloaking, four events each.

Two of the three ways the scan goes are a polite thank-you and a little scrap; the third is pirates holding the station hostage — two boarders aboard and a pirate ship on the radar. Blue options that pay here

  • Crew Teleporter ×4Clean entry to Zoltan trade hub, freed slaves at Slaver (friendly) at level 2, plus branches in Nebula lost ship and Capture the ship.
  • Cloaking ×4Three graded payoffs inside Malfunctioning defense system, and a way out of Auto-ship fight in plasma storm, Rebel fight choice in nebula and Auto-ship near storage station in nebula.
  • Medbay ×2Level 3 turns Zoltan research facility into a free drone; level 2 or 3 saves the man dying on the moon in Single life form on moon.
  • Slug ×2Talks the Zoltan security checkpoint guards out of searching you, and reads the madman's intentions in Single life form on moon.
  • Mind Control ×1Convinces the Zoltan security checkpoint checkpoint it already cleared you today, for the same fuel the Slug gets.
  • Boarding Drone ×1One drone part digs a Zoltan scientist out of the caves under Zoltan odd moon.

What you can leave with

  • CrewRuwen, a Crystal crew member, at Zoltan research facility — the guaranteed beacon, if you arrive carrying the Damaged Stasis Pod. The pod is spent doing it.
  • CrewZoltan from three places: dug out of Zoltan odd moon, hired for 40 scrap off Zoltan retake the ship (he may refuse), or handed over by Zoltan ship asks to dock after they shoot first and then call it a test you passed.
  • WeaponHealing Burst from Zoltan Great Eye — the only named item anywhere in this pool.
  • AugmentZoltan free augment is an academy handing one over for nothing. Zoltan retake the ship pays a better one, but only if you clear his ship without destroying it.
  • CrewMantis, Rockman or Engi — your pick — out of the hold of Slaver (friendly); a human named Charlie from Single life form on moon.
  • MapZoltan free map reveals the sector for free, on a tour of the ship you are not supposed to be reading the charts on.

What can bite

  • CrewFive events in the pool can take a crew member. Two of them — Zoltan security checkpoint and Zoltan Great Eye — do it in a way a Clone Bay cannot undo.
  • BoardersEight events put someone aboard, and three are Zoltan: 3–4 at Zoltan border police, 2–4 at both Zoltan security checkpoint and Zoltan trade hub.
  • SystemsRefusing to hand over a crew member at Zoltan security checkpoint costs you half your weapons before the shooting starts.
  • FleetPirate briber is the only event here that can buy a jump back from the pursuit. Auto-ship warning in nebula and Rebel fight choice in nebula each cost you one.
