Sector profile · ZOLTAN_SECTOR

Zoltan Controlled Sector

One beacon here is fixed: the Zoltan research facility, where a Damaged Stasis Pod becomes a Crystal crew member. Everything else is Zoltan bureaucracy — checkpoints, customs sweeps, docking permits, entry fees — and the fights that come out of it are mostly ones you agreed to. Only two of the seven events on the guaranteed-hostile list are actually Zoltan; the rest are pirates, Mantis, a jumpy Engi escort and the Rebel fleet flying under Zoltan flavour text.

The engine builds its candidate list from every species whose rarity here is not 0, weights each one 6 − rarity, and fills each of the three crew slots with its own independent draw — so a store can offer the same species twice. "Per slot" is that species' share of the total weight; "in a store" is the chance it turns up in at least one of the three slots.

Weighting read out of FTLGame_orig.exe, not from any data file: the candidate loop skips rarity 0 and computes 6 − rarity as the weight. The three-slot count is the Advanced Edition figure, which is the data these pages are built from; vanilla rolls two or three.

Left of the arrow is the blueprint's base rarity, right of it is this sector's. 1 is the commonest and 5 the rarest; 0 is a flag rather than a low number — that entry is not offered here at all. A sector that declares no rarity list of its own uses the base values throughout.

Per the community wiki, the number does two jobs: it weights what a store offers, and it gates event rewards without weighting them — nonzero here means a crew-kill reward can hand you that species, 0 means it never can. The game files state neither. No file anywhere on this machine gives the weighting function, so nothing on this page turns a rarity into odds.

Lines are filled in this order, and the map holds at most 24 beacons (19–24 by the community wiki's count). When they run out, generation stops — a line near the bottom can receive nothing at all.

This sector allocates up to 32 slots against 24 beacons, so the bottom of the table is a wish list rather than a promise.

Could be cut entirely if everything above rolls high: QUESTS_ZOLTAN, NEUTRAL_ZOLTAN.

Placed before everything else, whatever the file order, because the cloud graphics come first: NEBULA_ZOLTAN. A cloud drawn over an ordinary beacon converts it, and that beacon draws from the shared NEBULA list instead.

Beacons still empty at the end of the table are filled from the shared NEUTRAL list (OVERRIDE_NEUTRAL under Advanced Edition). That line is not in the file — the engine reaches the list by name — so it is marked rather than numbered in the table above.

Here that is up to 4 beacons: a full 24-beacon map, less the 20 this table must place.

The exit beacon is not in this table at all — it draws from the shared EXIT_LIST, and an exit inside a cloud is always empty.

Entry beacon is always START_BEACON_ZOLTAN.

The two lines at risk are the two that make this sector Zoltan. NEUTRAL_ZOLTAN is the checkpoints, the wise man, the docking requests and the briber; QUESTS_ZOLTAN is every quest that can start here. Roll high on clouds and hostiles and what is left is a shooting sector with two stores. Roll low instead — the table's floor is 20 slots — and the beacons nobody claimed do not come back to this list; they take generic filler.

Blue options that pay here

  • Zoltan crew ×1Of the 18 gated events in the pool, exactly one blue option asks for a Zoltan — the Zoltan trade hub entry fee, and it still costs 10 scrap. Flying Zoltan buys you nothing in Zoltan space.
  • Crew Teleporter ×4Clean entry to Zoltan trade hub, freed slaves at Slaver (friendly) at level 2, plus branches in Nebula lost ship and Capture the ship.
  • Cloaking ×4Three graded payoffs inside Malfunctioning defense system, and a way out of Auto-ship fight in plasma storm, Rebel fight choice in nebula and Auto-ship near storage station in nebula.
  • Medbay ×2Level 3 turns Zoltan research facility into a free drone; level 2 or 3 saves the man dying on the moon in Single life form on moon.
  • Slug ×2Talks the Zoltan security checkpoint guards out of searching you, and reads the madman's intentions in Single life form on moon.
  • Boarding Drone ×1One drone part digs a Zoltan scientist out of the caves under Zoltan odd moon.

What you can leave with

  • CrewRuwen, a Crystal crew member, at Zoltan research facility — the guaranteed beacon, if you arrive carrying the Damaged Stasis Pod. The pod is spent doing it.
  • CrewZoltan from three places: dug out of Zoltan odd moon, hired for 40 scrap off Zoltan retake the ship (he may refuse), or handed over by Zoltan ship asks to dock after they shoot first and then call it a test you passed.
  • WeaponHealing Burst from Zoltan Great Eye — the only named item anywhere in this pool.
  • AugmentZoltan free augment is an academy handing one over for nothing. Zoltan retake the ship pays a better one, but only if you clear his ship without destroying it.
  • CrewMantis, Rockman or Engi — your pick — out of the hold of Slaver (friendly); a human named Charlie from Single life form on moon.
  • MapZoltan free map reveals the sector for free, on a tour of the ship you are not supposed to be reading the charts on.

What can bite

  • CrewFive events in the pool can take a crew member. Two of them — Zoltan security checkpoint and Zoltan Great Eye — do it in a way a Clone Bay cannot undo.
  • BoardersEight events put someone aboard, and three are Zoltan: 3–4 at Zoltan border police, 2–4 at both Zoltan security checkpoint and Zoltan trade hub.
  • SystemsRefusing to hand over a crew member at Zoltan security checkpoint costs you half your weapons before the shooting starts.
  • FleetPirate briber is the only event here that can buy a jump back from the pursuit — and it sits in the line most likely to be cut. Auto-ship warning in nebula and Rebel fight choice in nebula each cost you one.

Reading the map here

  • StoresBoth fixed stores are settled early enough that nothing can squeeze them out. The two events that can open a shop on top of them — Pirate briber and Zoltan trade hub — are in the two lines that get cut, and neither is marked in advance.
  • DistressNothing leaks a distress marker in from another line here, so the markers you see are the distress line and nothing else. Eight events can be behind one, two of which can cost a crew member and one of which is Pirate ship distress trap.
  • CloudsSensors go dark inside one, but this is not a nebula sector, so a cloud beacon halves the fleet's advance for that jump. It is the cheapest delay on the map — and the clouds are drawn before the sector is populated, so their footprint is set before anything else is.
  • The exitNever one of the beacons below — exits draw from a list shared by every sector. If the clouds happen to cover it, the exit is simply empty.

Generation rules per the community's reverse-engineering of the map generator; the game files state none of them.

minSector in the files is zero-indexed, so the "earliest sector" chip is that value plus one — the number a player counts from the start of a run. The community wiki states the same offset.

The 24-beacon ceiling follows from the 6×4 grid; the 19-beacon floor is stated by the community wiki alone and is not derivable from anything else here, so the at-risk marks use the ceiling.

"Unique" is the files' own unique="true". Whether that means once per sector or once per run is not stated in any file here; the wiki's better bet is once per sector.

The shipped event lists carry no weights, so no beacon here has stated odds. A pool entry listed twice is counted as ×2 and marked.
