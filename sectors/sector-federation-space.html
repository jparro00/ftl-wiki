Sector profile · STANDARD_SPACE

Federation Space

The sector every run opens in, and the only table in the game with a line switched off: HOSTILE_BOARDING is allocated 0–0, eight boarding events with no beacon to put them on. The rest is the plainest pool there is — pirates, Rebel patrols and auto-scouts, with nothing named in any reward. Its maxima come to exactly the 24 beacons a map can hold, so a map that comes up even one beacon short starts eating the bottom of the table.

Solid blocks are the minimum the sector must place; faded blocks are the optional extra.

Lines are filled in this order, and the map holds at most 24 beacons (19–24 by the community wiki's count). When they run out, generation stops — a line near the bottom can receive nothing at all.

Could be cut entirely if everything above rolls high: HOSTILE_BOARDING.

Placed before everything else, whatever the file order, because the cloud graphics come first: NEBULA. A cloud drawn over an ordinary beacon converts it, and that beacon draws from the shared NEBULA list instead.

Beacons still empty at the end of the table are filled from the shared NEUTRAL list (OVERRIDE_NEUTRAL under Advanced Edition).

The exit beacon is not in this table at all — it draws from the shared EXIT_LIST, and an exit inside a cloud is always empty.

Entry beacon is always START_BEACON.

Versus the Civilian Sector, which draws the same lists at almost every line: that one gets 2–3 stores and 2–3 item beacons against this sector's 1–2 and 1, and up to 8 nebula beacons against 4, and its maxima run well past anything a map can hold. This is the trimmed-down version of that sector — less of everything except the quest line, which is 1–1 here against the Civilian Sector's 0–2, and a boarding line that exists but is empty where the Civilian Sector's is commented out of the file entirely.

Blue options that pay here

  • Crew Teleporter ×8The most-used gate in the sector. It is one of three keys into Capture the ship, lifts a survivor off Asteroid belt distress, and at level 2 tries to take a slave from Slaver (friendly) without paying — which can turn him hostile instead. One of the eight, Research station with no response, sits on the boarding line that places nothing; every gate count on this page includes those.
  • Cloaking ×5Graded at Malfunctioning defense system: the same choice pays low, medium or high at levels 1, 2 and 3.
  • Hacking ×4Auto-ship carrying shield virus halves your shield system if you have no answer. Hacking cancels the virus and turns it into an ordinary fight.
  • Engines ×4Level 6 is the alternative to handing a crew member over at Slaver (hostile) — it starts the fight on your terms rather than avoiding it.
  • Medbay ×4Both levels matter at Single life form on moon: level 2 saves the man, level 3 returns him with a level in every skill.
  • Rockman / Engi ×2Fire on research station pays a high-tier augment for a Rock crew member; Unknown disease on mining colony takes either species. In both events it is the plain choice that risks a crew member.

What you can leave with

  • Nothing namedNo event in this pool hands over a specific weapon, drone or augment — every reward is a generic tier roll. Crew and fleet time are what is actually worth routing for.
  • Crew, boughtCrew hiring station sells crew outright at 25–45 and 25–55 scrap. Slaver (friendly) sells one for 25–45.
  • Crew, freeSingle life form on moon is a free hand with a level in one skill — unless the roll gives you the madman instead, which costs a crew member.
  • Fleet timeThe mercenary sells two jumps of delay for 10–25 scrap, or the whole sector map for 10–20; beat the pirate at Pirate briber and the ship you saved can be leaned on for a jump instead of scrap. Those two are the only bought delay in the pool.
  • HullThe biggest repair in reach is on the hidden-base chain: one of its outcomes puts 35 hull back.

What can bite

  • Boarders14 events in the pool put boarders aboard, and 8 of them sit in the 0–0 list. The six that remain are Encrypted federation signal, Refugee comms down, Merchant's request and three nebula beacons.
  • Crew10 events can kill a crew member. Only two of them specify no clone: Slaver (hostile), where you hand one over, and Unknown disease on mining colony.
  • The fleetFive things in the pool push the fleet a jump closer: Rebel ship warning and Auto-ship warning on the fixed hostile pair, Auto-ship warning in nebula and Rebel fight choice in nebula in the cloud, and walking away from Asteroid belt distress if the civilians survive to report you.
  • SystemsCapture the ship asks you to take a ship intact. Destroying it instead costs 13 hull, a system, and fires — the payout only comes from killing the crew.

Reading the map here

  • StoresOne to two, and the store line is filled before anything else, so it is the one part of the table that never gets squeezed. Four events can open a shop on top of that — Pirate briber, Settlement mercenary work, Escort civilians and Escort civilians FTL haywire — and they are the only route to a third.
  • Distress markersThe 1–2 distress line does not determine how many markers you see. The same twelve tagged events also sit on the neutral line, which is filled earlier, so a map can carry more distress markers than the distress line allocates — and two events allocated as distress, Pirate ship attacking civilian distress and Rebel ship attacking Federation loyalists, carry no tag and never show one. Behind a marker here: a free crew member, a refuelling ship, a pirate trap, or Giant alien spiders.
  • The quest beaconNine events in the pool plant a quest marker and only six of them are on the quest line, so more quests than the line's single beacon is normal. Fewer is also normal: it is the last line before the empty boarding entry, so a map that fills early leaves the sector with no quest beacon at all. A marker also cannot be placed inside cloud, and with no room it is pushed into the next sector instead.
  • Inside a cloudClouds are drawn over the map, and an ordinary beacon caught under one is converted and drawn from this same nebula list — so cloud cover only ever deepens these sixteen. Each stop in cloud blinds your sensors and halves the fleet's advance for that jump; four of the sixteen are plasma storms, which cut your reactor in half on arrival, Plasma storm incapacitated ships among them.
  • Where the squeeze landsThe table's maxima stop exactly at the 24 beacons a map can hold, which is why only the empty boarding line is chipped as at-risk. That assumes every grid cell fills, which is the exception rather than the rule — on a smaller map the shortfall lands on whatever is at the bottom, which here is the quest beacon and the fixed hostile pair.

Generation rules per the community's reverse-engineering of the map generator; the game files state none of them.

minSector in the files is zero-indexed, so the "earliest sector" chip is that value plus one — the number a player counts from the start of a run. The community wiki states the same offset.

The 24-beacon ceiling follows from the 6×4 grid; the 19-beacon floor is stated by the community wiki alone and is not derivable from anything else here, so the at-risk marks use the ceiling.

"Unique" is the files' own unique="true". Whether that means once per sector or once per run is not stated in any file here; the wiki's better bet is once per sector.

The shipped event lists carry no weights, so no beacon here has stated odds. A pool entry listed twice is counted as ×2 and marked.
