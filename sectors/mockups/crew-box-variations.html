“Crew a store can sell here” — shorter

The block lists the species a store in this sector can stock. Today it is a five-column table of eight rows — weight, a bar, per-slot share, in-store chance — plus three greyed rows for species that can never appear. It is the tallest tile in the At a glance row and carries more digits than a glance can absorb.

Width. Every box below is rendered at 457px — the panel’s real rendered offsetWidth, measured in a headless browser on sector-federation-space-mock.html at a 1440×900 viewport. The .glance grid is auto-fit / minmax(19rem, 1fr) inside a 928px .wrap, which resolves to two columns of 456.8px. All heights below are measured at that width and are not comparable to the earlier 21rem pass.

Applied to every variation: the wt column is gone — weight is derived (6 − rarity) and no player acts on it, so per-slot and in-store carry the meaning alone. All percentages are rounded to whole numbers. Bar and segment geometry still uses the unrounded values, so a bar and its label can differ by up to half a point. Variation 0 is deliberately untouched — it is the current panel, decimals and weight column included, because a baseline that has already been improved measures nothing.

What the numbers mean (SECTOR-PAGE.md §4.3c): weight = 6 − rarity; per slot = weight ÷ 17, one slot’s chance; in store = 1 − (1 − share)³, the chance the species shows up in at least one of the three slots. Rarity 0 means the species is not in the pool at all. Nothing below states an odd this data does not give.

Header treatments — its own axis

The <h3> is a fixed cost paid by every body treatment below. At 457px it no longer wraps, so the saving is smaller than it looked at 21rem — but the count still competes with the title for the eye, and pushing it to the far edge (today’s justify-content: space-between) is what makes the line read as two things instead of one.

  • All four are the same single line at 457px, so the axis buys no measurable height — it buys reading speed. The choice is about what the eye does first, not about px.
  • HA splits the line in two and is the only one that needs a return sweep to read. HB reads as one phrase; it is the right default and is used on V2–V8 below.
  • HC pairs only with bodies that show the never-sold species explicitly (V4, V6) — the “5 of 8” is then visible in the box and the count is redundant. On V3, V5 or V8 it hides that three species were excluded, which is exactly the fact worth keeping.
  • HD pairs best with the wide bodies (V4, V5): the rule fills the space HB leaves empty, and “3 slots” — the one piece of context a player cannot derive — sits where the numbers begin.

Variation 1 — The same rows, two columns …

The baseline’s own row — species, per-slot, in-store — with the weight column removed and the percentages rounded, then folded down the middle so the list is four rows deep instead of eight. Nothing about how a row reads changes; only how many of them stack.

  • Drops the weight column and the decimals, and nothing else. Keeps the baseline’s row shape, both figures, all eight species, and the greyed .out treatment the current box already uses for species a store here cannot sell.
  • Reads down, then across. Left column is Human, Engi, Mantis, Rockman; right is Zoltan and the three never-sold. Across-then-down would pair Human with Engi on one line and scatter the never-sold three across two rows; column-major keeps the existing top-to-bottom rank scan intact and lands all three excluded species contiguously in the lower right, where they read as one block rather than three interruptions. The split also falls on a real boundary in the data — everything at 44% or better is on the left, everything at 17% or 0% on the right.
  • Two sub-headers, one per group, rather than one spanning header — see V1-alt below for the measured comparison. Each group’s labels sit directly over its own numbers, which a spanning header cannot do across a 1.1rem gutter.
  • Abbreviated labels. A half-width column cannot hold “per slot” / “in store” at the .chead tracking without overflowing 2.9rem, so the sub-headers read slot and store, with the full phrase on each as a tooltip. That is the one wording change; the figures are unchanged.
