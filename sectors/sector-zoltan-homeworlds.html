Sector profile · ZOLTAN_HOME

Zoltan Homeworlds

Both named beacons sit at the head of the queue and are placed before anything is rolled: a Zoltan peace envoy that opens a ship unlock with no crew, system or equipment requirement anywhere in it, and a research facility that turns a Damaged Stasis Pod into a Crystal crew member. What the sector cannot promise is everything underneath them — it asks for more slots than any sector except the two Slug nebulas, and the three lines it can lose whole are its items, its quests and its neutral pool. Two stores are guaranteed, Zoltan is the most common crew on their shelves, and the rest is bureaucracy with guns.

The engine builds its candidate list from every species whose rarity here is not 0, weights each one 6 − rarity, and fills each of the three crew slots with its own independent draw — so a store can offer the same species twice. "Per slot" is that species' share of the total weight; "in a store" is the chance it turns up in at least one of the three slots.

Weighting read out of FTLGame_orig.exe, not from any data file: the candidate loop skips rarity 0 and computes 6 − rarity as the weight. The three-slot count is the Advanced Edition figure, which is the data these pages are built from; vanilla rolls two or three.

Left of the arrow is the blueprint's base rarity, right of it is this sector's. 1 is the commonest and 5 the rarest; 0 is a flag rather than a low number — that entry is not offered here at all. A sector that declares no rarity list of its own uses the base values throughout.

Per the community wiki, the number does two jobs: it weights what a store offers, and it gates event rewards without weighting them — nonzero here means a crew-kill reward can hand you that species, 0 means it never can. The game files state neither. No file anywhere on this machine gives the weighting function, so nothing on this page turns a rarity into odds.

Solid blocks are the minimum the sector must place; faded blocks are the optional extra. Open a line to see every event it can place; open an event to read its card here.

Lines are filled in this order, and the map holds at most 24 beacons (19–24 by the community wiki's count). When they run out, generation stops — a line near the bottom can receive nothing at all.

This sector allocates up to 33 slots against 24 beacons, so the bottom of the table is a wish list rather than a promise.

Could be cut entirely if everything above rolls high: ITEM_ZOLTAN, QUESTS_ZOLTAN, NEUTRAL_ZOLTAN.

Placed before everything else, whatever the file order, because the cloud graphics come first: NEBULA_ZOLTAN. A cloud drawn over an ordinary beacon converts it, and that beacon draws from the shared NEBULA list instead.

Beacons still empty at the end of the table are filled from the shared NEUTRAL list (OVERRIDE_NEUTRAL under Advanced Edition). That line is not in the file — the engine reaches the list by name — so it is marked rather than numbered in the table above.

Here that is up to 3 beacons: a full 24-beacon map, less the 21 this table must place.

The exit beacon is not in this table at all — it draws from the shared EXIT_LIST, and an exit inside a cloud is always empty.

Entry beacon is always START_BEACON_ZOLTAN.

Versus a plain Zoltan Controlled Sector: the allocation tables are otherwise identical — every other entry appears in both at the same min and the same max. ZOLTAN_HOME adds exactly one thing, the guaranteed Unarmed Zoltan transport beacon, and is unique="true" with minSector="2" where the plain sector is neither. Zoltan research facility is guaranteed in both, so the Crystal step is not what makes this sector special — the envoy is. That one extra beacon is also what costs the bottom of the table: the plain sector puts only its quests and its neutral pool at risk, this one puts its item line there too.

Blue options that pay here

  • Crew Teleporter ×4Beams you into Zoltan trade hub past the ID check entirely, and opens branches at Slaver (friendly), Nebula lost ship and Capture the ship.
  • Cloaking ×4Graded one through three at Malfunctioning defense system, and a way out of Auto-ship fight in plasma storm, Rebel fight choice in nebula and Auto-ship near storage station in nebula.
  • Slug ×2Whatever your Slug says to the guards at Zoltan security checkpoint, it ends with them handing over fuel and waving you through. Also opens Single life form on moon, and sees enemy crew inside the cloud.
  • Mind Control ×1The other clean pass at Zoltan security checkpoint — the guards believe they already checked you, and pay fuel for wasting your time.
  • Zoltan ×1One Zoltan aboard turns entry to Zoltan trade hub into a 10-scrap citizen's fee instead of a forged ID card and a countdown.
  • Damaged Stasis Pod ×1The augment is consumed at Zoltan research facility and a Crystal crew member walks out of it.

What you can leave with

  • CrewThree separate routes to a Zoltan: Zoltan ship asks to dock on a surrender, Zoltan retake the ship for 40 scrap, Zoltan odd moon for one drone part — all three on lines that may not be placed.
  • CrewA Crystal crew member at Zoltan research facility — but only if you arrive already carrying the pod. It is the one beacon outside the Crystal sector that hands one over.
  • AugmentZoltan free augment hands one over with no choice and no cost, and Zoltan retake the ship pays a high augment reward for taking the ship back without destroying it.
  • WeaponHealing Burst is one of the four things Zoltan Great Eye can do to you.
  • MapZoltan free map reveals the sector for nothing, and Pirate briber can push the fleet back a jump.
  • StoresTwo are guaranteed, and Zoltan is the most common crew type on the shelf here — the only sector besides the plain Zoltan one where that is true.

What can bite

  • FightsFourteen events open fire on arrival and another twenty-three can turn into a fight below a choice — most of the pool ends in combat if you engage with it.
  • CrewFive events can cost a crew member. Zoltan security checkpoint asks you to hand one to Zoltan law and Zoltan Great Eye simply unmakes one; both losses are flagged clone="false".
  • BoardersEight events put someone aboard — three to four Zoltan at Zoltan border police alongside a warship, up to five humans at Boarders: Humans jammed sensors.
  • SystemsRefusing to surrender the flagged crew member at Zoltan security checkpoint halves your weapon system and beams two to four Zoltan aboard in the same breath.

Reading the map here

  • DistressHonest for once: nothing outside the distress line carries the tag in this sector, so a distress marker is one of exactly eight events — and the line is filled early enough that at least one always exists.
  • StoresTwo are labelled from the start and cannot be squeezed out. A third can come from Zoltan trade hub or Pirate briber, both on lines that may never be placed, or from a trade station at the exit, which is drawn from a list shared with every sector.
  • NebulaTwo to six beacons of cloud, and this is not a nebula sector — so each one halves the Rebel advance for the jump into it, the full slowdown rather than the reduced one. The price is blind sensors, and an ion storm on any cloud beacon the fleet takes.
  • Quest markersA marker can never be planted on a nebula beacon, a store, a distress beacon or one you have already visited.
