Zoltan Controlled Sector

One beacon here is fixed: the Zoltan research facility, where a Damaged Stasis Pod becomes a Crystal crew member. Everything else is Zoltan bureaucracy — checkpoints, customs sweeps, docking permits, entry fees — and the fights that come out of it are mostly ones you agreed to.

Ten of the fourteen events here arrive through NEBULA_REBEL, so most of this block is the generic Rebel nebula pool rather than anything Zoltan. Two to six is a floor rather than a total — the clouds are painted before any other line is filled, and ordinary beacons they cover become nebula beacons drawing from a pool this sector never lists. The standout is Zoltan Great Eye: approaching the monolith gives you the Healing Burst, a heap of scrap, nothing at all, a fight, or one crew member aged backwards out of existence.

The one beacon the sector must place, and nothing above it can crowd it out. Two of the three ways the scan goes are a polite thank-you and a little scrap; the third is pirates holding the station hostage — two boarders aboard and a pirate ship on the radar. Must be placed — every solid block is filled, as long as the map still has a free beacon when the line comes up.
May be placed — the line rolls one count between its minimum and its maximum and every outcome is equally likely. NEBULA_ZOLTAN rolls 2–6, so each of those 5 counts is 20%. A faded block is the chance of at least that many: 80% for the first faded one, 20% for all 6. Hover any block for its own figure.
Beacons that always put you in a fight.

The map is a 6×4 grid and each cell has an 80% chance of holding a beacon, so a sector has at most 24 of them. The lines above are then filled in that order, each rolling its own count, until the beacons run out — so a line near the bottom can get nothing. Whatever is still empty at the end is filled from the shared NEUTRAL list.

Clouds are drawn before any of it. A beacon under one is converted and draws from NEBULA instead.

Blue options that pay here

  • Zoltan crew ×1Of the 18 gated events in the pool, exactly one blue option asks for a Zoltan — the Zoltan trade hub entry fee, and it still costs 10 scrap. Flying Zoltan buys you nothing in Zoltan space.
  • Crew Teleporter ×4Clean entry to Zoltan trade hub, freed slaves at Slaver (friendly) at level 2, plus branches in Nebula lost ship and Capture the ship.
  • Cloaking ×4Three graded payoffs inside Malfunctioning defense system, and a way out of Auto-ship fight in plasma storm, Rebel fight choice in nebula and Auto-ship near storage station in nebula.
  • Medbay ×2Level 3 turns Zoltan research facility into a free drone; level 2 or 3 saves the man dying on the moon in Single life form on moon.
  • Slug ×2Talks the Zoltan security checkpoint guards out of searching you, and reads the madman's intentions in Single life form on moon.
  • Boarding Drone ×1One drone part digs a Zoltan scientist out of the caves under Zoltan odd moon.

What you can leave with

  • CrewRuwen, a Crystal crew member, at Zoltan research facility — the guaranteed beacon, if you arrive carrying the Damaged Stasis Pod. The pod is spent doing it.
  • CrewZoltan from three places: dug out of Zoltan odd moon, hired for 40 scrap off Zoltan retake the ship (he may refuse), or handed over by Zoltan ship asks to dock after they shoot first and then call it a test you passed.
  • WeaponHealing Burst from Zoltan Great Eye — the only named item anywhere in this pool.
  • AugmentZoltan free augment is an academy handing one over for nothing. Zoltan retake the ship pays a better one, but only if you clear his ship without destroying it.
  • CrewMantis, Rockman or Engi — your pick — out of the hold of Slaver (friendly); a human named Charlie from Single life form on moon.
  • MapZoltan free map reveals the sector for free, on a tour of the ship you are not supposed to be reading the charts on.

What can bite

  • CrewFive events in the pool can take a crew member. Two of them — Zoltan security checkpoint and Zoltan Great Eye — do it in a way a Clone Bay cannot undo.
  • BoardersEight events put someone aboard, and three are Zoltan: 3–4 at Zoltan border police, 2–4 at both Zoltan security checkpoint and Zoltan trade hub.
  • SystemsRefusing to hand over a crew member at Zoltan security checkpoint costs you half your weapons before the shooting starts.
  • FleetPirate briber is the only event here that can buy a jump back from the pursuit — and it sits in the line most likely to be cut. Auto-ship warning in nebula and Rebel fight choice in nebula each cost you one. Against them the clouds are the cheapest brake: this is not a nebula sector, so a cloud beacon halves the advance for the jump into it, at the price of blind sensors while you sit there.
