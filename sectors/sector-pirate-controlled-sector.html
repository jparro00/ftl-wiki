Sector profile · PIRATE_SECTOR

Pirate Controlled Sector

Six to eight beacons here are a fight — the biggest single line the sector asks for — and exactly one more puts boarders on your ship, every run, no exceptions. The rest of the table is a wish list: it asks for more events than the map has room for, and because the neutral line is filled last, the deepest and most interesting pool in the sector is the one that gets cut. What is guaranteed is the shooting.

Solid blocks are the minimum the sector must place; faded blocks are the optional extra. Open a line to see every event it can place; open an event to see its card.

Lines are filled in this order, and the map holds at most 24 beacons (19–24 by the community wiki's count). When they run out, generation stops — a line near the bottom can receive nothing at all.

This sector allocates up to 29 slots against 24 beacons, so the bottom of the table is a wish list rather than a promise.

Placed before everything else, whatever the file order, because the cloud graphics come first: NEBULA_PIRATE. A cloud drawn over an ordinary beacon converts it, and that beacon draws from the shared NEBULA list instead.

Beacons still empty at the end of the table are filled from the shared NEUTRAL list (OVERRIDE_NEUTRAL under Advanced Edition).

The exit beacon is not in this table at all — it draws from the shared EXIT_LIST, and an exit inside a cloud is always empty.

Entry beacon is always START_BEACON_PIRATE.

Versus Rebel Controlled space: the same nine lines, in the same order, with the same numbers — one difference, Rebel space allocates 0–2 quests against this sector's 0–1. What changes between them is which lists those lines point at, not how many beacons anything gets.

Blue options that pay here

  • Crew Teleporter ×6The most-used gate in the sector, and the only way into the boarding half of Slaver (friendly) and Nebula lost ship.
  • Cloaking ×4Graded outs across the malfunctioning defense system, the plasma storm and the undetected Rebel — level 1 gambles, level 2 is clean, level 3 is better again.
  • Hacking ×3Both events that hack you have a Hacking counter: the engine lockdown in Pirate engine hacker, and the sensor jam in Boarders: Humans jammed sensors that otherwise leaves you at zero sensors with boarders already aboard.
  • Medbay ×4Levels 2 and 3 both matter — Single life form on moon, Unknown disease on mining colony, and the advanced-Medbay cure inside Research station with no response that turns a boarding party into a recruit.
  • Sensors 2 / Long-Ranged ScannersScans the crates in Destroyed cargo ship before you bring them aboard — the same event that, unscanned, is a 2-in-4 chance of boarders. Neither reads anything inside the cloud.
  • Weapon Control 6Turns Pirate smuggler into a bribe you can pocket, and buys a warning shot at Pirate ship attacking civilian distress that half the time scares the pirate off the civilian entirely.

What you can leave with

  • AugmentDamaged Stasis Pod at Dense asteroid field distress — the only named item in the entire pool, and it sits in the line the map cuts first.
  • Crew34 events in this sector can put someone new on your ship. The slavers are the reliable ones: Slaver (hostile) and Slaver (friendly) both offer a Mantis, Rockman or Engi, one by buying, one by killing.
  • CrewA human from Single life form on moon, and paid hires at Crew hiring station.
  • Stores1–2 guaranteed reads thin. It is not: four more events in the pool can open a shop on top of them, and the Advanced Edition item list adds a fifth. This is not the sector that starves you of places to spend.
  • Quests6 events plant a marker. Beyond the merchant chain, Escort civilians FTL haywire and Escort civilians both feed the same escort target, and two more point at the hidden Federation base.
  • Scrap41 of the 66 events are flagged unique, so the sector rewards jumping wide — repeats are far more likely to be the fights, which have no such flag.

What can bite

  • Boarders9 events put people inside your hull and one of them is guaranteed. Refugee comms down is the worst-value version: five outcomes behind one "prepare to board", one of which is 2–4 humans and another of which just kills a crew member.
  • Crew9 events can cost you someone. Giant alien spiders, Fire on research station and Plasma storm incapacitated ships are the ones with no cheap way out if you arrive unequipped.
  • RansomPirate toll charges 15–25 scrap to be allowed to leave, and Slaver (hostile) charges a crew member. Refusing either is a fight you did not choose the ground for.
  • BaitPirate ship distress trap is a distress call that is a pirate, Destroyed cargo ship is free cargo that is boarders, and Pirate briber pays you to watch someone else get robbed. The distress channel is not information in this sector.

Reading the map here

  • The squeezeOn a map that fills all 24 cells, every line above the neutral pool rolling its maximum still leaves one beacon for a line that wants five or six. The stores, the fights and the boarding beacon are never the ones cut; the variance in this sector lives in its last two lines.
  • DistressThe surplus distress markers all ride on the neutral line, which is filled after the distress line rather than before it — so here the extras are exactly what gets cut when the map fills. What you can see from one jump away is most often one of the ten drawn from the distress list itself, and Pirate ship distress trap sits in both.
  • The cloud0–5 beacons are drawn into nebula before any other line is placed. In a sector that is not itself a nebula, sitting in one halves the Rebel advance for that jump — a cloud-heavy roll is free time on the clock, paid for with dead sensors while you are in it.
  • Quest markersA marker has to land on a beacon that is not a store, not a distress beacon, not already visited and not inside the cloud. Roll the nebula line high and that shortlist can come up empty, at which point the quest is handed to the next sector instead of this one.
  • Buying timeThe mercenary sells 2 jumps of delay for 10–25 scrap, or a revealed map for 10–20; Pirate briber can talk a rescued Rebel scout into 1. Against them, Auto-ship warning in nebula and Rebel fight choice in nebula advance the fleet if the scout gets its message out, and Asteroid belt distress costs a jump to take.
  • HazardsFour beacons in the pool sit in an asteroid field or beside a sun, three more in a plasma storm that halves your reactor, and Advanced Edition adds a pulsar. While any of those is live the ship menu is locked — no upgrades, no weapon swaps, no moving crew — so the power you jumped in with is the power you fight with.

Generation rules per the community's reverse-engineering of the map generator; the game files state none of them.

minSector in the files is zero-indexed, so the "earliest sector" chip is that value plus one — the number a player counts from the start of a run. The community wiki states the same offset.

The 24-beacon ceiling follows from the 6×4 grid; the 19-beacon floor is stated by the community wiki alone and is not derivable from anything else here, so the at-risk marks use the ceiling.

"Unique" is the files' own unique="true". Whether that means once per sector or once per run is not stated in any file here; the wiki's better bet is once per sector.

The shipped event lists carry no weights, so no beacon here has stated odds. A pool entry listed twice is counted as ×2 and marked.

Defined in the files both as an event list and as a single event, and read here as the list: BOARDERS_PIRATE, NEBULA_PIRATE.
