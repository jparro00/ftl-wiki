Slug Home Nebula

Purple usually means a breather. Here a cloud beacon shaves only about a fifth off the Rebel advance instead of halving it, your sensors are dead across most of the map, and the Slugs open a fight by switching one of your systems off before they shoot.

Must be placed — every solid block is filled, as long as the map still has a free beacon when the line comes up.
May be placed — the line rolls one count between its minimum and its maximum and every outcome is equally likely. ITEMS rolls 0–2, so each of those 3 counts is 33%. A faded block is the chance of at least that many: 67% for one, 33% for all 2. Hover any block for its own figure.
Beacons that always put you in a fight.

The map is a 6×4 grid and each cell has an 80% chance of holding a beacon, so a sector has at most 24 of them. The lines above are then filled in that order, each rolling its own count, until the beacons run out — so a line near the bottom can get nothing. Whatever is still empty at the end is filled from the shared NEUTRAL list.

Clouds are drawn before any of it. A beacon under one is converted and draws from NEBULA instead.

NEUTRAL is a numbered line here as well as the fill-in row — one pool, reached two ways, not a doubled row.

Blue options that pay here

  • Hacking ×6The single best system to fly in with. It cancels the remote hack in Slug hacker (doors), Slug hacker (oxygen), Slug hacker (medical) and Slug hacker (choice) — four fights that otherwise open with a system already at zero — and sweetens Auto-ship near storage station in nebula and Pirate ship selling drones on top.
  • Slug ×6The crewman who cancels the sector's own blackout, and the game says so in his description. A telepathic duel that can end The Black Raven without a shot, the ambush spotted early in Slug store ship, the dying survivor located in Nebula wreckage, an Engi out of Intelligent ponies with no dice roll attached — and the quiet route through the unlock chain.
  • Sensors ×7Level 2 or 3 opens a branch in six events, Rebel fight chance in nebula, Deactivated Auto-ship, Battlefield wreckage and Terraforming scan among them — and level 2 is the non-Slug way through the unlock chain, where the text has you overclocking the system to punch through the clouds.
  • Medbay 2 ×3Keeps the system half-alive under the hack in Slug hacker (medical), saves the dying survivor in Nebula wreckage for a crew member, and cures the infected crewman in Plagued station.
  • Cloaking ×3Clean exits from Rebel fight choice in nebula, Auto-ship near storage station in nebula and Auto-ship near storage station. Hiding from that Rebel patrol without it can instead lose you a jump to the fleet.
  • Mantis / RockmanOne crew member each turns a Slug trap around: the Mantis walks into Slug oxygen malfunction expecting it, and the Rockman drinks whatever is in the flask at Slug drink on your behalf.

Named things you can leave with

  • WeaponAnti-Bio Beam — the surrender offer at Slug Home Nebula surrender. It is the same choice as the unlock chain, so you get one or the other.
  • CrewA named Slug crew member from Slocknog — he asks 55 scrap, and sometimes waives it if you start powering up the FTL instead.
  • CrewA Slug at Slug moons question: the arrival text tells you how many moons the planet has, and that is the answer. Guessing costs scrap, fuel and drone parts.
  • CrewAn Engi at Intelligent ponies, a human at Plagued station, and a Mantis, Rockman or Engi at Slaver (friendly).
  • WeaponAn unnamed weapon for 45 scrap from Pirate ship selling weapon, who may also take the scrap and hand over nothing. Mind Control gets him talking about what it actually is.

What can bite

  • SystemsThe hacker family is the real hazard: doors locked open next to fire weapons, oxygen cut, medbay dark with boarders already aboard, or shields, oxygen or weapons halved by your own answer at Slug hacker (choice).
  • BoardersSix events put someone on your ship. Slug hacker (medical) lands two Slugs while your medbay is off, and Boarders: Humans in plasma storm drops three to four humans on you with no ship fight attached.
  • ScrapSlug repair station patches your hull and then clamps you to the station: 15 fuel or 50 scrap. Refusing means a planted bomb, damage and a fight anyway.
  • CrewOnly three events in the pool can kill a crew member — Slug oxygen malfunction, Intelligent ponies and Plagued station. This sector takes systems and scrap far more often than it takes people.
  • FuelThe empty entries can take up a lot of the map between them, and Empty nebula beacon (Slug) is the larger of the two. Every one of those is a jump spent for nothing, in a sector with two stores you can rely on.
  • Flying blindSensors do nothing at a cloud beacon, and most of this map is cloud: no enemy rooms, no enemy crew, no weapon charge bars. A Slug in the crew or a Lifeform Scanner is what puts the enemy back on your screen — both are written specifically to work where sensors do not.
