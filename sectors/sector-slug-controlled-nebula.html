Sector profile · SLUG_SECTOR

Slug Controlled Nebula

Slug space does not open a fight, it opens with one of your systems already switched off — your doors, your oxygen, your medbay, or whichever of shields, oxygen and weapons you are made to nominate. A Hacking system is the one blue option that answers all four, and the clouds take your sensors away before you can see any of it coming. The usual consolation for flying blind is cut down here too: a nebula beacon in a nebula sector shaves only about a fifth off the Rebel advance, where the same beacon anywhere else would halve it.

The engine builds its candidate list from every species whose rarity here is not 0, weights each one 6 − rarity, and fills each of the three crew slots with its own independent draw — so a store can offer the same species twice. "Per slot" is that species' share of the total weight; "in a store" is the chance it turns up in at least one of the three slots.

Weighting read out of FTLGame_orig.exe, not from any data file: the candidate loop skips rarity 0 and computes 6 − rarity as the weight. The three-slot count is the Advanced Edition figure, which is the data these pages are built from; vanilla rolls two or three.

Left of the arrow is the blueprint's base rarity, right of it is this sector's. 1 is the commonest and 5 the rarest; 0 is a flag rather than a low number — that entry is not offered here at all. A sector that declares no rarity list of its own uses the base values throughout.

Per the community wiki, the number does two jobs: it weights what a store offers, and it gates event rewards without weighting them — nonzero here means a crew-kill reward can hand you that species, 0 means it never can. The game files state neither. No file anywhere on this machine gives the weighting function, so nothing on this page turns a rarity into odds.

Solid blocks are the minimum the sector must place; faded blocks are the optional extra. Open a line to see every event it can place; open an event to read its card here.

Lines are filled in this order, and the map holds at most 24 beacons (19–24 by the community wiki's count). When they run out, generation stops — a line near the bottom can receive nothing at all.

This sector allocates up to 34 slots against 24 beacons, so the bottom of the table is a wish list rather than a promise.

Could be cut entirely if everything above rolls high: DISTRESS_BEACON_SLUG, STORM_SLUG, NEUTRAL.

Placed before everything else, whatever the file order, because the cloud graphics come first: NEBULA_STORE_SLUG, NEBULA_NOTHING_SLUG, NEBULA_HOSTILE_SLUG, NEBULA_NEUTRAL_SLUG. A cloud drawn over an ordinary beacon converts it, and that beacon draws from the shared NEBULA list instead.

Beacons still empty at the end of the table are filled from the shared NEUTRAL list (OVERRIDE_NEUTRAL under Advanced Edition). That line is not in the file — the engine reaches the list by name — so it is marked rather than numbered in the table above.

Here that is up to 6 beacons: a full 24-beacon map, less the 18 this table must place.

NEUTRAL appears twice above because this sector is one of the few that also names it as a numbered line — the same pool, reached two ways.

The exit beacon is not in this table at all — it draws from the shared EXIT_LIST, and an exit inside a cloud is always empty.

Entry beacon is always START_BEACON_SLUG.

Versus Slug Home Nebula: the same eleven lines with the same counts, plus one guaranteed NEBULA_SLUG_FIGHT_UNLOCK beacon — and that sector is once per run where this one can repeat. Everything on this page otherwise describes both.

Blue options that pay here

  • Hacking ×6The sector's answer key. It counters every Slug system hack — Slug hacker (doors), Slug hacker (oxygen), Slug hacker (medical) and Slug hacker (choice) — though the counter zeroes your own hacking system for the duration of that fight.
  • Slug ×5A mind duel instead of a battle at The Black Raven, the teleporting boarder spotted early at Slug store ship, the survivor found at Nebula wreckage, a conversation at Intelligent ponies, and a better price at Pirate ship selling drones. A Slug aboard also reads enemy crew straight through the fog, which is the one thing the clouds cannot take from you.
  • Sensors ×6Worth less than the count suggests, because the clouds switch sensors off. Only Rebel fight chance in nebula offers a sensors branch inside them, and its text admits you are squeezing it out of malfunctioning gear; the other five sit in the non-nebula lists.
  • Scanners ×2Long-Ranged Scanners work where sensors do not — Nebula lost ship and Rebel fight chance in nebula both say outright that you cannot track anything through the nebula without them. A Lifeform Scanner opens the same branch on the second.
  • Medbay ×3Level 2 saves the plague victim at Plagued station, keeps half your medbay powered through Slug hacker (medical) instead of losing it entirely, and turns the dying survivor into crew.
  • Mind Control ×1Talks Pirate ship selling weapon down on price. One of its two outcomes has him come to his senses and attack instead.

What you can leave with

  • CrewA free Slug at Slug moons question if you count the moons correctly — the text names the number. Getting it wrong costs 35 scrap, fuel and drone parts.
  • CrewSlocknog at Slocknog asks 55 scrap. Ignore him once and he offers to come along for nothing.
  • CrewAn Engi from Intelligent ponies, a Human from Plagued station, and Mantis, Rock or Engi for sale at Slaver (friendly).
  • WeaponA high weapon reward from The Black Raven for winning the duel or taking his surrender; a random weapon for 45 scrap from Pirate ship selling weapon, where one of two outcomes takes the scrap and hands over nothing.

What can bite

  • SystemsThe hacker trio does not damage a system, it switches it off: doors, oxygen production, or medbay and clone bay both. Slug hacker (choice) halves shields, oxygen or weapons at your choice, or takes 35 scrap to go away.
  • PowerA plasma storm halves your reactor on arrival, rounding up, and strips power off your systems by itself — shields can be down before anything has fired. Zoltan bars and a Backup Battery are the exceptions, and the enemy reactor is halved too.
  • BoardersSix events put someone aboard — up to four at Refueling platform, three to four humans at Boarders: Humans in plasma storm with no ship to shoot back at, two Slugs teleported in before the fight even starts at Slug hacker (medical).
  • CrewOnly three events can kill a crew member outright: Slug oxygen malfunction, Plagued station and Intelligent ponies — and the last two sit in NEUTRAL, which also mops up every beacon the table failed to fill, so they turn up more often than a 1–2 line suggests.
  • ScrapSlug repair station repairs you and then presents a bill for 50 scrap or 15 fuel. Refusing to pay starts a fight with your weapons halved or 4 hull already gone, and a third docking outcome caps your engines at 1 before the shooting even begins.
  • SalesSlug store ship is a real store on one of three outcomes. The other two board you, and one of them locks your weapons off first.

Reading the map here

  • StoresTwo of the three are guaranteed and are placed before anything else, so the squeeze never reaches them. Three more events can open a shop on top of that — Pirate briber, Slug drink and Slug store ship.
  • QuestsA quest marker cannot be placed on a nebula beacon, a store, a distress beacon or the exit — and the four cloud lines claim twelve to eighteen beacons before anything else is placed. Nebula wreckage and Slug comm tapping are both inside the clouds, and a marker with nowhere to land is pushed into the next sector instead.
  • The fleetNebula cover is worth less at home than abroad. A cloud beacon here leaves the Rebels advancing about four fifths of a normal jump; the same beacon in a non-nebula sector cuts them to a half.
  • DistressOf the five distress events that raise a marker here, only Pirate ship distress trap reads as a possible ship on Long-Ranged Scanners. The other four read as an unvisited location — which is not the same as safe: two of them can still end in a fight.
  • Caught in a cloudA nebula beacon taken by the fleet always gains an ion storm, but never an Anti-Ship Battery unless you are sitting there out of fuel. Being overtaken in the fog costs you your reactor rather than your hull.
  • The exitExits draw from a list shared by every sector, and an exit that ends up under the cloud graphics is simply empty — no event, and no ion storm even once the fleet has taken it.
