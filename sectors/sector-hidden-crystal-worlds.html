Hidden Crystal Worlds

The sector you cannot fly to: a Crystal crewmember brings you, and the ship-unlock marker is planted on the arrival beacon before you have made a single choice. It is also the only sector whose minimum demand is larger than the biggest map the game can build, so it always leaves something unplaced — and always the same something, off the bottom of the table.

Twelve is the ask; what it gets is the map's leftovers — at most eleven, and fewer every time a line above rolls high. This is where the sector's arithmetic is paid from. Three of the ten are a third party shooting at a Crystal ship (Rebel ship attacking Crystal ship, Mantis ship attacking Crystal, Pirate ship attacking Crystal), where stepping in can be paid off with a Crystal weapon. Federation deserters is the outlier: 15–25 scrap and 1–3 fuel to a Federation ship that ran, in exchange for the map.

Beacons that always put you in a fight.

The map is a 6×4 grid and each cell has an 80% chance of holding a beacon, so a sector has at most 24 of them. The lines above are then filled in that order, each rolling its own count, until the beacons run out — so a line near the bottom can get nothing. Whatever is still empty at the end is filled from the shared NEUTRAL list.

Clouds are drawn before any of it. A beacon under one is converted and draws from NEBULA instead.

This table asks for 25 beacons against the 24 a map can hold, so the bottom of it is cut every time.

Blue options that pay here

  • Crystal ×2Your own Crystalline Being retunes the ship's weapons and opens Crystalline cache with no roll at all, and turns Crystal chat into free supplies.
  • Breach Missiles ×1The other clean way into the cache — one missile spent, instead of firing everything you have and mostly bouncing off.
  • Engines 7 · Teleporter 2Both pull your crewmember back out of the cache's singularity trap. Engines keep the cargo as well; the teleporter saves the person only.
  • Rockman · Backup DNA BankTwo ways through Crystalline research facility without putting a body on the table — the Rockman is an evolutionary cousin, the DNA bank is data they have never seen.
  • Distraction Buoys ×1Worth carrying in twice over: it postpones the fleet a turn as you arrive, and at Crystalline ship messaging about Rebels it takes the scrap and the jump of delay with none of the chance of being caught faking it.
  • Four events, seven gatesOnly four events in the whole pool gate anything, and Crystalline cache carries four of the seven gates by itself.

Named things you can leave with

  • WeaponHeavy Crystal Mark II from Crystalline men buried — but only by sending a crewmember down to the dig and then waiting out two full cycles.
  • WeaponCrystal Lockdown Bomb or Crystal Burst Mark II from Crystal scrap collector: pay the collector 35 scrap, then ask for payment in kind rather than taking him aboard.
  • CrewA Crystal crewmember two ways — that same collector, or accepting the surrender at Crystal fight and agreeing to take one of the survivors.
  • CrewA Human, out of the cargo hold of the ship that was hunting them, if Crystal fight with surrender offer (Human crew) surrenders.
  • WeaponA Crystal weapon off the cache at Crystalline cache, and off rescuing the ship under fire at Rebel ship attacking Crystal ship, Mantis ship attacking Crystal or Pirate ship attacking Crystal.
  • HullCrystal fight with surrender offer (hull repairs) repairs 8 if you stop the fight once the escort falters; Crystal chat repairs 6 on one of the three ways the conversation can go.

What can bite

  • CrewThree events can cost a crewmember and all three are your own choice: the singularity in Crystalline cache, the dig at Crystalline men buried, and letting the scientists at Crystalline research facility run their tests.
  • BoardersThree events put Crystalline crew on your ship — two to three at Boarders: Crystal and again if the cache's owners are home, one or two at Crystalline ship messaging about Rebels if they catch you lying.
  • FleetCrystalline men buried advances the Rebel fleet a jump for every cycle you wait, and the Heavy Crystal is behind two of them. Rebel ship attacking Crystal ship advances it one if you shoot the Crystal ship rather than the Rebel.
  • FuelThe cache escape that saves both the crewmember and the cargo detonates your entire fuel reserve to do it. Advanced Engines do the same job for nothing.
  • RefusalsCrystalline research facility can answer a flat no by undocking a warship that will not accept surrender. Crystalline men buried is safe to decline outright — it only turns hostile if you promise help and then pull your crewmember back out.
  • Nothing to see comingNo event in the sector sets an environment — no asteroid field, no sun, no pulsar anywhere in the pool — so the only hazard here is the one the Rebels bring with a beacon they have taken, and Long-Ranged Scanners have nothing to warn you about. There is no cloud to duck into either, so the universal way of halving the fleet's advance is off the table.
