Engi Homeworlds

The friendliest sector in the game with a Mantis problem. Two to three stores, five item beacons, and the only place the Stealth Cruiser quest can begin — but not one hostile beacon here is Engi. What shoots at you is pirates, Rebel patrols, and Mantis raiders picking Engi stations apart.

Blue options that pay here

  • Engi ×5Starts the ship-unlock quest, and adds an out to the virus, the Mantis-attacked station, the smashed ships and the defense satellite.
  • Sensors ×7Level 2 or 3 opens branches across the wreckage, the asteroid belt, the federation signal and both Engi station events.
  • Scanners ×4Long-Ranged Scanners, including the one that makes Engi research station safe to work.
  • Cloaking ×3Escape routes out of the Mantis fight, the storage station and the defense system.
  • Hacking ×2Three graded payoffs inside The Engi virus — the higher the level, the better the deal.
  • Lanius ×1A clean solve on The Engi virus, with no fight and no loss.

Named things you can leave with

  • AugmentDamaged Stasis Pod — the Crystal route starts at Dense asteroid field distress.
  • CrewCrystal crew member at Zoltan research facility, if you are already carrying the pod.
  • AugmentEngi Med-bot Dispersal, and the Healing Burst bomb, both from Engi distress Rebel fight.
  • AugmentDrone Reactor Booster from The Engi virus, behind Hacking.
  • CrewAn Engi with two levels in every skill — the reborn virus, if you let The Engi virus run its course.
  • CrewEngi crew from Engi ship attacked by Mantis ship and Engi research station; Mantis from Mantis fugitive and Confused Mantis.

What can bite

  • CrewFive events in the pool can kill a crew member outright, Giant alien spiders and Engi research station among them.
  • SystemsThe Engi virus can halve your shields and your engines before the fight even starts.
  • BoardersSix events put someone aboard — up to four at Refueling platform, two to three humans on Encrypted federation signal.
  • MissilesEngi cache trades two missiles for two jumps of fleet delay. Cheap if you are not flying missiles.
