Civilian Sector

Nobody's territory, and the friendliest allocation a run is likely to get: two to three stores, two to three item beacons, and an item list in which not one event can hurt you. The fixed cost is six to eight hostile beacons — two of them Rebel scouts you arrive already shooting at — and the shape of the map is not fixed at all, because the nebula entry is allocated anywhere from zero to eight beacons and nothing else in the sector swings that far.

Versus Federation Space: the same lists under the same names, with the counts turned up — stores 1–2 becomes 2–3, items 1–1 becomes 2–3, nebula 0–4 becomes 0–8, and the guaranteed quest beacon becomes 0–2. The hostile allocation is identical in both, so what grows here is the shopping and the nebula, not the danger. The one list Federation Space carries that this sector does not is HOSTILE_BOARDING — allocated 0–0 there, and commented out entirely here, so neither sector places a boarding beacon by this table.

Blue options that pay here

  • CoverageThirty-one of the seventy-seven events in the pool carry at least one gated option, across thirty-four distinct requirements. Most of those requirements are one-offs; the ones below are what repeats.
  • Crew Teleporter ×7The most-wanted gate in the sector. It lifts the survivors out of Asteroid belt distress, rescues the loyalists in Rebel ship attacking Federation loyalists, frees slaves at Slaver (friendly) at level 2, and is one of three keys to Capture the ship.
  • Cloaking ×5Auto-ship near storage station, Auto-ship near storage station in nebula, Auto-ship fight in plasma storm, Rebel fight choice in nebula and Malfunctioning defense system — which is graded, and pays differently at cloak level 1, 2 and 3.
  • Hacking ×3 · Engines ×3Hacking answers Auto-ship carrying shield virus, Pirate engine hacker and Auto-ship near storage station in nebula. Engines 6 is the escape from Slaver (hostile), and 4 is enough to outrun the scout in Rebel fight choice in nebula.
  • Rockman · EngiTwo distress events apiece. Both species answer Unknown disease on mining colony; a Rockman also walks into Fire on research station, an Engi into Malfunctioning defense system.
  • Medbay · Clone BaySingle life form on moon pays on Medbay 2, Medbay 3 or Clone Bay 2. Unknown disease on mining colony takes Medbay 2 or the Engi Med-bot Dispersal augment.

What you can leave with

  • Crew, 42 waysForty-two of the seventy-seven events in the pool have a branch that adds a crew member — more than half the sector. This is where a short-handed ship goes to fill up.
  • Crew, for saleCrew hiring station runs two listings, 25–45 and 25–55 scrap. Slaver (friendly) sells a freed slave for 25–45; a level-2 teleporter takes one instead of paying and starts a fight.
  • Crew, by speciesMantis, Rockman or Engi — your pick of the three — if you clear the crew off either slaver ship. A Human from Single life form on moon, on the branch a Slug opens.
  • Drone partsMerchant's request hands over five before you have done anything for them.
  • Hull35 points in one stop at the hidden Federation base. Single life form on moon and Asteroid belt distress both repair 10 on the take-them-home branch.
  • No named gearNothing in this pool names a specific weapon, drone or augment. Equipment arrives as an untyped reward roll, or through the stores.

What can bite

  • Crew, eight waysEight events can kill a crew member. Giant alien spiders is the sharpest of them — sending people in is a straight roll between a dead crew member and a high reward, unless you are carrying an Anti-Personnel Drone, a Boarding Drone or an Anti-Bio Beam.
  • Boarders ×5Refugee comms down is the trap: a drifting refugee ship with its comms down, and two to four humans aboard. Boarders: rebels in nebula and Boarders: Humans in plasma storm are three to four, with no ship to shoot at.
  • The fleetFive events can hand the Rebels a jump: Rebel ship warning, Auto-ship warning and Auto-ship warning in nebula if the scout escapes, Rebel fight choice in nebula if hiding goes wrong, and Asteroid belt distress for walking away from the rescue.
  • TollsPirate toll wants 15–25 scrap to let you through, and refusing starts the fight it was threatening anyway.
