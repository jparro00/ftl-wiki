Sector profile · CIVILIAN_SECTOR

Civilian Sector

Nobody's territory, and a genuine shopping sector: two to three guaranteed stores — matched only by the Engi sectors and the Crystal homeworld — plus two to three item beacons on a list where not one event can hurt you. The fixed cost is six to eight hostile beacons, two of them Rebel scouts you arrive already shooting at. What it will not promise is a quest: this sector is allowed to roll zero, and the nebula line ahead of the quest line is wide enough to leave nothing for it.

The engine builds its candidate list from every species whose rarity here is not 0, weights each one 6 − rarity, and fills each of the three crew slots with its own independent draw — so a store can offer the same species twice. "Per slot" is that species' share of the total weight; "in a store" is the chance it turns up in at least one of the three slots.

Weighting read out of FTLGame_orig.exe, not from any data file: the candidate loop skips rarity 0 and computes 6 − rarity as the weight. The three-slot count is the Advanced Edition figure, which is the data these pages are built from; vanilla rolls two or three.

Solid blocks are the minimum the sector must place; faded blocks are the optional extra. Open a line to see every event it can place; open an event to read its card here.

Lines are filled in this order, and the map holds at most 24 beacons (19–24 by the community wiki's count). When they run out, generation stops — a line near the bottom can receive nothing at all.

This sector allocates up to 32 slots against 24 beacons, so the bottom of the table is a wish list rather than a promise.

Could be cut entirely if everything above rolls high: QUESTS, HOSTILE1.

Placed before everything else, whatever the file order, because the cloud graphics come first: NEBULA. A cloud drawn over an ordinary beacon converts it, and that beacon draws from the shared NEBULA list instead.

Beacons still empty at the end of the table are filled from the shared NEUTRAL list (OVERRIDE_NEUTRAL under Advanced Edition). That line is not in the file — the engine reaches the list by name — so it is marked rather than numbered in the table above.

Here that is up to 10 beacons: a full 24-beacon map, less the 14 this table must place.

The exit beacon is not in this table at all — it draws from the shared EXIT_LIST, and an exit inside a cloud is always empty.

Versus Federation Space: the same lists under the same names with the counts turned up — stores 1–2 becomes 2–3, items 1–1 becomes 2–3, nebula 0–4 becomes 0–8. One line runs the other way. Federation Space guarantees exactly one quest beacon; here the quest line is 0–2 and sits at the bottom of a table that asks for more than the map can hold, so the richer sector is also the one that can leave you with no quest beacon at all. The only list Federation Space carries that this sector does not is HOSTILE_BOARDING — allocated 0–0 there, commented out entirely here, so neither sector places a boarding beacon by this table.

Blue options that pay here

  • CoverageThirty-one of the seventy-seven events in the pool carry at least one gated option, across thirty-four distinct requirements. Most of those requirements are one-offs; the ones below are what repeats.
  • Crew Teleporter ×7The most-wanted gate in the sector. It lifts the survivors out of Asteroid belt distress, rescues the loyalists in Rebel ship attacking Federation loyalists, frees slaves at Slaver (friendly) at level 2, and is one of three keys to Capture the ship.
  • Cloaking ×5Auto-ship near storage station, Auto-ship near storage station in nebula, Auto-ship fight in plasma storm, Rebel fight choice in nebula and Malfunctioning defense system — which is graded, and pays differently at cloak level 1, 2 and 3.
  • Hacking ×3 · Engines ×3Hacking answers Auto-ship carrying shield virus, Pirate engine hacker and Auto-ship near storage station in nebula. Engines 6 is the escape from Slaver (hostile), and 4 is enough to outrun the scout in Rebel fight choice in nebula.
  • Rockman · EngiTwo distress events apiece. Both species answer Unknown disease on mining colony; a Rockman also walks into Fire on research station, an Engi into Malfunctioning defense system.
  • Medbay · Clone BaySingle life form on moon pays on Medbay 2, Medbay 3 or Clone Bay 2. Unknown disease on mining colony takes Medbay 2 or the Engi Med-bot Dispersal augment.

What you can leave with

  • Crew, 42 waysForty-two of the seventy-seven events in the pool have a branch that adds a crew member — more than half the sector. This is where a short-handed ship goes to fill up.
  • Crew, for saleCrew hiring station runs two listings, 25–45 and 25–55 scrap. Slaver (friendly) sells a freed slave for 25–45; a level-2 teleporter takes one instead of paying and starts a fight.
  • Crew, by speciesMantis, Rockman or Engi — your pick of the three — if you clear the crew off either slaver ship. A Human from Single life form on moon, on the branch a Slug opens.
  • Drone partsMerchant's request hands over five before you have done anything for them.
  • Hull35 points in one stop at the hidden Federation base. Single life form on moon and Asteroid belt distress both repair 10 on the take-them-home branch.
  • No named gearNothing in this pool names a specific weapon, drone or augment. Equipment arrives as an untyped reward roll, or through the stores.

What can bite

  • Crew, eight waysEight events can kill a crew member. Giant alien spiders is the sharpest of them — sending people in is a straight roll between a dead crew member and a high reward, unless you are carrying an Anti-Personnel Drone, a Boarding Drone or an Anti-Bio Beam.
  • BoardersSix events put someone aboard. Refugee comms down is the trap: a drifting refugee ship with its comms down, and two to four humans aboard. Boarders: rebels in nebula and Boarders: Humans in plasma storm are three to four, with no ship to shoot at.
  • The fleetFive events can hand the Rebels a jump: Rebel ship warning, Auto-ship warning and Auto-ship warning in nebula if the scout escapes, Rebel fight choice in nebula if hiding goes wrong, and Asteroid belt distress for walking away from the rescue. Against them, The mercenary sells two jumps of delay and Pirate briber one.
  • TollsPirate toll wants 15–25 scrap to let you through, and refusing starts the fight it was threatening anyway.

Reading the map here

  • StoresTwo to three are guaranteed and nothing can squeeze them out. Four events in the pool can open a shop on top of that — Pirate briber, Settlement mercenary work, Escort civilians and Escort civilians FTL haywire — and the Advanced Edition item list adds a fifth in Large trade station. A store marker on the map is not necessarily one of the guaranteed ones.
  • Distress markersDo not read the one-to-two distress allocation as the number of diamonds you will see. The neutral line carries the whole distress list inside it and is filled earlier, so neutral beacons can come out as distress markers too. Twelve events can be sitting behind one, and five of those can cost a crew member.
  • Quest markersNine events in this pool plant a marker, but a marker needs a beacon that is unvisited, is not a store, is not already a distress signal and is not inside a cloud. In a sector that can lay eight nebula beacons and three stores, those exclusions bite hardest: a quest accepted here has fewer legal beacons to land on than one accepted in a sector with no clouds, and a quest with nowhere to go is handed to the next sector.
  • Inside the cloudsEvery nebula beacon blinds your sensors and halves the Rebel advance for that jump; the plasma-storm ones halve your reactor the moment you arrive, so leave spare power before jumping. Clouds never carry an anti-ship battery, which makes them the least bad place to be overtaken — the fleet turns them into an ion storm instead.
  • Both ends of the rangeRoll every line low and the sector asks for far fewer beacons than the map holds, and the leftovers come from the shared filler list that no section below shows. Roll every line high and it asks for more than the map holds and the bottom of the table never happens. The same sector can be a tight tour of its own lists or a stretch of generic filler.
