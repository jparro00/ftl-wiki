The Last Stand

The one sector whose table cannot fill the map, so nothing on it is ever cut and the spare beacons draw from the galaxy-wide fallback list instead. What it places is supply and repetition — three repair stations, one store, and the same Rebel scout on every hostile beacon; the fight you came for is put on the map by the endgame, not by this table.

What the sector hands you

  • RepairRepair station in Last Stand is a fixed three beacons: 15 hull, every damaged system restored, 4 missiles, 5 fuel, 5 drone parts and 22–44 scrap, each time.
  • On arrivalEntering the sector pays 10 fuel and repairs 10 hull before you have jumped anywhere.
  • HullRebel ship attacking civilians in Last Stand can repair 8 more hull — one of three outcomes after you save the cruiser, the others being thanks and a bundle of supplies.
  • FuelRebel ship attacking refueling outpost pays a fuel bonus from the grateful outpost on top of the salvage, but only if you choose to intervene.
  • CrewTwo events can hand you a crew member, Fight in Last Stand and Rebel fight, and only through the dead-crew salvage — the prisoner is found aboard a ship whose crew you killed, not one you blew up.
  • StoreExactly one, allocated 1–1, and the last place to spend scrap before the Flagship.

The endgame map

  • No exitThis is the only sector with no exit beacon at all, so the shared exit list that fills one everywhere else never runs. Nothing on this map is a way out.
  • Where the base sitsToward the middle-right of the grid, and the game places it a fixed distance from your arrival — four jumps away on easy and normal, five to six on hard. The Flagship spawns in the two right-most columns, four to six jumps from the base.
  • A different clockThe fleet does not sweep in from the left here. It takes scattered individual beacons each turn, and the Flagship takes whatever it is sitting on — so danger arrives behind you as readily as ahead.
  • Waiting is a moveYou can hold at a beacon with fuel still in the tank. It ticks the map forward like a jump, and a fight that starts after a wait begins with your FTL already charged.

What can bite

  • Six forced fightsFight in Last Stand fills every hostile beacon, and the ship it loads has no surrender and no escape branch. Every one of the six ends in a destroyed hull or a dead crew.
  • Overtaken beaconsA beacon the fleet takes loses whatever was on it — including a repair station you were saving — and becomes an Elite Fighter under an anti-ship battery, paying one fuel for the kill. There is nothing to farm there.
  • Thin payRebel fight among Federation and Rebel fleets is fighting on arrival and pays a low, scrap-only reward when destroyed — the text says outright there is no time to salvage the wreck.
  • RunnersRebel fight can surrender or power up its FTL and leave, so a beacon that looks like scrap can end with neither the fight nor the salvage.
  • Attrition, not disasterNo event in this pool puts boarders aboard and none can kill a crew member. What the sector takes is hull and ammunition, across the six guaranteed fights plus whatever the neutral pool adds.

What is not here

  • Blue optionsNone. Not one event in the pool has a gated choice, so no system level, augment or crew species changes a single outcome in this sector.
  • QuestsNo quest line is allocated and nothing in the pool plants a marker — this sector cannot hold a quest at all. A quest you trigger in the sector before it is not carried forward either; it is simply dropped.
  • Unlocks and lootNothing here unlocks a ship or hands out a named weapon or augment.
  • SpeciesNo event in the pool names a crew type, and the sector declares no rarity list of its own — nothing in this sector's data tunes what the store's crew slots offer.
  • UniqueRebel ship attacking refueling outpost is the only event here carrying the files' own unique flag.
