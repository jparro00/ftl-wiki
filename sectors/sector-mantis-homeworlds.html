Sector profile · MANTIS_HOME

Mantis Homeworlds

The Mantis sector with a name in it. Six or seven beacons open in combat before you can choose anything, one or two more skip the ship fight entirely and drop 2–4 boarders straight onto your deck, and one beacon is always Legendary thief KazaaakplethKilik — the only ship unlock either Mantis sector can hand you. Arrive repaired and stocked: shops are as scarce here as in pirate or Rebel space, and no beacon in this sector slows the Rebel fleet.

Solid blocks are the minimum the sector must place; faded blocks are the optional extra. Open a line to see every event it can place; open an event to read its card here.

Lines are filled in this order, and the map holds at most 24 beacons (19–24 by the community wiki's count). When they run out, generation stops — a line near the bottom can receive nothing at all.

This sector allocates up to 27 slots against 24 beacons, so the bottom of the table is a wish list rather than a promise.

Beacons still empty at the end of the table are filled from the shared NEUTRAL list (OVERRIDE_NEUTRAL under Advanced Edition).

The exit beacon is not in this table at all — it draws from the shared EXIT_LIST, and an exit inside a cloud is always empty.

Entry beacon is always START_BEACON_MANTIS.

Versus a plain Mantis Controlled Sector: every list is the same list and every count is the same count — 6–7 hostile, 6–7 neutral, 1–2 boarding, 1–3 distress, 1–2 items, 1–2 stores, 2–3 empty. The whole difference is the guaranteed MANTIS_NAMED_THIEF beacon, and because it is taken at the top of the table it costs nothing above it — what it displaces, on a full map, is one more neutral encounter at the bottom. One more event in the pool, and with it the sector's only ship unlock, its only named augment and its third quest start.

Blue options that pay here

  • Adv. Medbay / Adv. Clone BayThe only two keys to the whole thief payout. Medbay 2 also opens the safe answer on Unknown disease on mining colony.
  • Sensors ×3Level 3 throughout: it finds the dying thief without boarding, reads Deactivated Auto-ship before you wake it, and adds a line at Trade scrap for upgrades.
  • Cloaking ×2Slips past the sleeping warship in Mantis fight choice without a fight, and is graded at levels 1–3 on Malfunctioning defense system.
  • Rockman ×2 · Engi ×2All four sit in the distress pool — Fire on research station, Unknown disease on mining colony and Malfunctioning defense system. The crew that pays in the Mantis homeworlds is not Mantis.
  • Drones ×4Anti-Personnel, Boarding and Anti-Bio Beam each answer Giant alien spiders; a System Repair Drone answers Fire on research station.
  • Mantis ×1One blue option in the entire sector needs a Mantis crew member, and it changes only the flavour text on Legendary thief KazaaakplethKilik.

Named things you can leave with

  • AugmentMantis Pheromones — the only named item anywhere in the pool, and it comes off the thief.
  • CrewKazaaakplethKilik, a Mantis with two levels in every skill, on the revive branch.
  • ShipunlockShip id="2", transmitted with the same handshake that hands you the crew and the augment.
  • CrewMantis from Escape pod and Mantis fugitive; the same escape pod can hold a human instead; Crew hiring station sells one for scrap.
  • WeaponsTwo of the sector's three quest starts pay a weapon at the beacon they mark: the thief's MANTIS_NAMED_THIEF_STASH, and DONOR_MANTIS_CHASE2 if you chase the collectors down.

What can bite

  • BoardersFour events put hostiles inside your hull, and the map guarantees at least one boarding beacon. Boarders: Mantis and Boarders: Humans near sun arrive 2–4 strong with no ship fight attached.
  • CrewFour events can kill a crew member outright: Giant alien spiders, Fire on research station, Unknown disease on mining colony, and Escape pod, which kills and leaves the killer aboard.
  • FightsSeven events in the pool open in combat and eight more can turn into it from a choice — and that is before the boarding pool, which never involves an enemy ship at all.
  • StarsMantis fight near sun and Boarders: Humans near sun both stage next to a star, with the solar hazard running on your ship while you fight or repel.
  • TrapsPirate ship distress trap is a distress call that opens hostile, and siding with the fugitive in Mantis fugitive can cost hull and a system before the Engi ship even opens fire.

Reading the map here

  • DistressOne of the few sectors whose distress markers are honest: every event in the distress list carries the tag and no event outside it does, so what the table allocates is exactly what the map can show you.
  • A marked shopEscort civilians FTL haywire is the sector's one unplanned store, and it is a distress beacon — so the shop you did not expect arrives wearing the distress marker, not the store marker.
  • The fleetNo nebula line anywhere in the table, so nothing here halves the Rebel advance and every jump costs full pursuit. The compensation is that sensors work at every beacon in the sector.
  • Quest markersThree events plant one, and a marker can only take over an ordinary unvisited beacon — never a store, a distress beacon or the exit. Trigger them early: with the fleet close behind, the marker is pushed into the next sector instead.

Generation rules per the community's reverse-engineering of the map generator; the game files state none of them.

minSector in the files is zero-indexed, so the "earliest sector" chip is that value plus one — the number a player counts from the start of a run. The community wiki states the same offset.

The 24-beacon ceiling follows from the 6×4 grid; the 19-beacon floor is stated by the community wiki alone and is not derivable from anything else here, so the at-risk marks use the ceiling.

"Unique" is the files' own unique="true". Whether that means once per sector or once per run is not stated in any file here; the wiki's better bet is once per sector.

The shipped event lists carry no weights, so no beacon here has stated odds. A pool entry listed twice is counted as ×2 and marked.
