Sector profile · REBEL_SECTOR

Rebel Controlled Sector

Rebel space, held by the fleet that is already chasing you — and the only place where that pursuit stops being background weather and becomes something events touch. Seven of the 49 events here move the fleet clock, and six of them can only move it the wrong way: a scout that lives long enough to charge its FTL reports your position. Against that there is a hostile floor of six beacons and a boarding you cannot decline, which makes this a sector you cross rather than one you farm.

The map is not one map. The sector must place 16 beacons and may place 30, and NEBULA_REBEL alone accounts for five of that fourteen-beacon swing — the same list that carries all three plasma-storm beacons and the three-to-four-strong boarding party. A nebula-heavy roll is a meaner sector than a nebula-free one, and nothing in the allocation stops either.

Blue options that pay here

  • Cloaking ×4The default way out: past the storage-cache guard in clear space and in nebula, out of the plasma storm, and clean past the Rebel in Rebel fight choice in nebula. Only Auto-ship near storage station rolls for detection no matter what level you are carrying.
  • Crew Teleporter ×5Boards the sensor station without provoking its guard, pulls the lost Federation crew out of the nebula, and is one of four ways to save the loyalists at Rebel ship attacking Federation loyalists — the one that gets you a crew member and the base coordinates.
  • Hacking ×2Auto-ship near radar station is the only reliable brake on the pursuit in the whole pool: the Hacking branch delays the fleet a jump and reveals the map for one drone part, where the plain hack is a four-way roll that can trip an alarm instead.
  • Engines ×2Pure escape, and graded — level 3 is a coin flip out of the plasma storm, level 6 is certain, level 4 loses the Rebel inside the cloud at Rebel fight choice in nebula.
  • Sensors ×6The widest gate in the sector once the quest stages are counted. Level 3 reads the sensor station's map from a distance and scans Deactivated Auto-ship instead of waking it; level 2 and Long-Ranged Scanners both salvage the Federation base's dead end.
  • Rock crew / Repair droneFire on research station kills a crew member on anyone who walks in unaided. A Rockman walks through the fire for a high augment reward; a System Repair Drone does the same job for a drone schematic.

What you can leave with

  • Crew ×23Twenty-three of the forty-nine events can hand you someone — by a distance the most common thing this sector gives, and mostly prisoners and survivors pulled out of ships you have just beaten.
  • Weapons & dronesRebel transport ship is the richest single roll in the pool — eleven destroyed-outcomes covering military weaponry, a drone schematic, drone parts, a map reveal and a prisoner.
  • Augments ×2Only Fire on research station behind Rock crew and Asteroid mining colony for fifteen missiles produce one, and neither names it — both roll a random augment.
  • HullThe hidden Federation base's space-dock outcome repairs 35, the largest patch anything in reach of this sector offers. Short of that it is Repair station at 5, 10 or 20 for scrap and Asteroid mining colony at 5 to 15 for missiles.
  • A RebelRebel defector is the only event in the pool that names the species it gives you — a human, three times out of six, sometimes with a scrap cache attached.

What can bite

  • The pursuit ×6Auto-ship warning, Rebel ship warning and Auto-ship warning in nebula if the scout escapes; Rebel fight choice in nebula if it spots you and lives; Rebel defector if the defector was lying; Asteroid belt distress if you leave the civilians to die and they survive to report you.
  • Boarders ×6One beacon of them is guaranteed, but six events can put people aboard: up to four humans at Boarders: Humans near sun, Boarders: rebels in nebula and the infected station on Merchant's request, two to three at Rebel fight with boarders and Encrypted federation signal, one or two at Rebel defector.
  • Crew loss ×4Plasma storm incapacitated ships, Fire on research station, Rebel defector and Merchant's request are the only events that can take someone off the roster, and every one of them is a branch you opt into — nothing here costs crew on arrival.
  • Drone partsAuto-ship near radar station charges a drone part for the approach whether or not it works, including the outcome where the drone is shot down and the auto-ship comes for you anyway.
  • BaitPirate ship distress trap is a distress call with a pirate behind it, and one of the five outcomes at Encrypted federation signal is a Rebel signal broadcast specifically to catch ships like yours.
