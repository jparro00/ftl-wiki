Sector profile · ROCK_HOME

Rock Homeworlds

The Rockmen fight you at home, and they fight you in weather — five of the eight events across the hostile and boarder pools open in an asteroid field or beside a sun, hazards that lock the ship menu for as long as you stay. You come anyway for the two beacons the sector must place by name: one opens the Rock Cruiser quest, and Ancient device is the only door in the game to the Crystal worlds — for a ship that already has a Crystal aboard.

The way out of the game's normal map, and the Crystal crew blue option is the only choice that opens it — the Damaged Stasis Pod on its own will not do. Nothing in this sector can turn one into the other: the pod becomes a Crystal crew member at a Zoltan research facility, and there is none here, so whether this beacon pays was settled a sector or two ago. Without a Crystal aboard the device is a gamble: scrapping it either pays out a large pile of scrap or brings a Rock warship down on you for the vandalism. Carrying Ruwen is also the only thing that makes this beacon visible in advance — he turns it into a quest marker you can see from anywhere on the map.

Must be placed — every solid block is filled, as long as the map still has a free beacon when the line comes up.
May be placed — the line rolls one count between its minimum and its maximum and every outcome is equally likely. HOSTILE_ROCK rolls 6–8, so each of those 3 counts is 33%. A faded block is the chance of at least that many: 67% for the first faded one, 33% for all 8. Hover any block for its own figure.
Beacons that always put you in a fight.

The map is a 6×4 grid and each cell has an 80% chance of holding a beacon, so a sector has at most 24 of them. The lines above are then filled in that order, each rolling its own count, until the beacons run out — so a line near the bottom can get nothing. Whatever is still empty at the end is filled from the shared NEUTRAL list.

Clouds are drawn before any of it. A beacon under one is converted and draws from NEBULA instead.

Blue options that pay here

  • Sensors ×5Level 2 or 3 opens a branch at five separate beacons, the widest gate in the pool — Asteroid belt distress, Rebel ship attacking Federation loyalists, Encrypted federation signal, Rock atheists and Trade scrap for upgrades.
  • Crew Teleporter ×4Slaver (friendly), Asteroid belt distress, Rebel ship attacking Federation loyalists and Capture the ship.
  • Rockman ×3Mantis ship with Rock body parts, Fire on research station and Unknown disease on mining colony all take a Rock crew member's word where yours does not count.
  • Rock Plating ×3Mantis ship with Rock body parts, Dense asteroid field distress and Asteroid belt distress — the augment the unlock chain pays out at its last step, spent straight back into this sector's pools.
  • System Repair Drone ×3Mantis ships battle for Rock freighter, Asteroid belt distress, and sending it into the flames at Fire on research station instead of a crew member.
  • Crystal ×1One event, and it is the one that matters: Ancient device.

Named things you can leave with

  • AugmentDamaged Stasis Pod at Dense asteroid field distress — the only named item the entire pool hands out.
  • AugmentA random augment for delivering the bride to Numa V, if you start Rock bride and go through with it.
  • CrewRockmen three ways: converted at Rock atheists, bought at Slaver (friendly), or kept as Ariadne by refusing the wedding.
  • CrewSlaver (friendly) is the one beacon that can produce a Mantis, a Rockman or an Engi; Crew hiring station sells crew outright; Dr Jones comes off the burning station at Fire on research station.
  • Crew28 of the 52 events in the pool can add a crew member — more than half the sector.
  • ReactorA reactor bar for 10–15 scrap at Rock and Slug standoff: pay off the Rock ship's debt and the Slugs sell you the power.

What can bite

  • CrewFive events can kill a crew member outright, and four of them are packed into a distress pool that only gets one or two beacons: Giant alien spiders, Fire on research station, Unknown disease on mining colony and Single life form on moon.
  • BoardersFour events put someone aboard. Three are Rockmen — up to three of them at Rock fight with boarders and at Boarders: Rockmen near sun — and Encrypted federation signal sends two to three humans.
  • AsteroidsEach rock either strips one shield layer or takes 1 hull and the system in that room, they keep coming after the enemy is dead, and they arrive faster the more shield levels you own. Rock fight in asteroid field, Rock pirates fight in asteroid field and Rock fight with boarders in asteroid field.
  • Solar flaresA flare every 28–34 seconds with five seconds of warning: one or two fires while shields hold, three to six when they are down, and every burning room can take hull and system damage with it. Rock pirates fight near sun and Boarders: Rockmen near sun.
  • AmmoRock live mine costs one drone part to cut away, or one missile and 3 hull to detonate. Send someone out to defuse it by hand and both wire choices end the same way: the crew member dies, 5 hull, a breach. Engines 5 backs you out of it for nothing.
  • ScrapDemand payment instead of paying at Rock and Slug standoff and it opens with 3 hull damage, a system knocked out, and a fight. Freeing a slave at Slaver (friendly) runs 25–45 scrap.
