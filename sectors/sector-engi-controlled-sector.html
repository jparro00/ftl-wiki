Sector profile · ENGI_SECTOR

Engi Controlled Sector

The Engi sector you actually meet — no depth requirement, no once-per-run limit, so a run can fly through two of them. The pools are generous: three beacons of Engi hand-outs, two to three stores, and a hostile list with no Engi ship anywhere in it. What it does not have is a named beacon — nothing here is placed by id, so there is no ship unlock and no guaranteed quest start.

Blue options that pay here

  • Engi crew · 4 eventsThe virus, the smashed ships, the Mantis-held station and the defence satellite. It is the only way to get the base marker out of Engi ship attacked by Mantis ship, and on The Engi virus it is the branch that gets a crewman killed.
  • Hacking · 2 eventsThree graded outs inside The Engi virus — quarantine it, or reprogram it for 15 hull and a reactor bar, or 30 hull at level 3, all three ending with the augment. Also the only alternative to crippled engines in Pirate engine hacker.
  • Sensors · 7 eventsThe widest gate in the sector. Level 2 tells you what is aboard the burning station in Engi research station before you commit, which turns its coin-flip into a clean crew pickup; level 2 or 3 also opens Battlefield wreckage, Deactivated Auto-ship and Engi ship attacked by Mantis ship.
  • Long-Ranged Scanners · 4 eventsAll three events that can plant the hidden-base marker — Encrypted federation signal, Asteroid belt distress and Engi ship attacked by Mantis ship — plus Engi research station.
  • Cloaking · 3 eventsWays out of Mantis fight choice, the storage station in Auto-ship near storage station and the satellite in Malfunctioning defense system, which grades its payoff by cloak level.
  • Lanius crew · 1 eventOne clean solve on The Engi virus — scrap and the augment, no fight and nobody lost.

What you can leave with

  • AugmentDamaged Stasis Pod from Dense asteroid field distress — the Crystal route starts here, and there is no fight anywhere under it. Searching plainly costs 4 hull and an engine hit; Rock Plating searches for free.
  • CrewA Crystal crew member at Zoltan research facility, if you arrive already carrying the pod.
  • Augment · WeaponEngi Med-bot Dispersal from Engi distress Rebel fight for 40 scrap, 2 missiles and 2 fuel; the Healing Burst bomb is one of three draws off the cheaper 25-scrap option.
  • AugmentDrone Reactor Booster from The Engi virus — every Hacking and Lanius branch hands it over.
  • CrewAn Engi with two levels in every skill: take the Engi option on The Engi virus, lose the crewman to the virus, win the fight, and it walks back out.
  • ScrapFree scrap with resources (Engi) and Engi surrender are free standard rewards with nothing asked in return — two of the six events on the three Engi item beacons.

What can bite

  • CrewFive events can cost you a crew member: Giant alien spiders, Engi research station, Confused Mantis, Merchant's request, and the Engi branch of The Engi virus.
  • BoardersSix events put someone aboard — up to four at Refueling platform, three to four at Merchant's request, two at Zoltan research facility, Mantis from Engi ship attacked by Mantis ship and Mantis fugitive, two to three humans on one draw of Encrypted federation signal.
  • SystemsThe Engi virus halves your shields and engines before the fight if you try to purge it yourself, and Pirate engine hacker holds engines at one bar until the pirate is dead.
  • The fleetRebel ship warning is a fight with no choice attached, and letting the scout jump away costs a jump of your lead. Pirate briber can buy one back, but only on the branch where you fight the pirate and the victim turns out to be a Rebel scout.
