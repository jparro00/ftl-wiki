# Fandom — "Rebel fight (Slug)"

## Summary
Community wiki page for `SLUG_REBEL`, retrieved at revision 73790. The Rebel counterpart to
[[source-fandom-pirate-fight-slug]] and equally minimal — three intro variants and
"default rewards".

## Key Takeaways
- Names the in-game id in Trivia: *"This event is called 'SLUG_REBEL' in the
  datafiles."*
- Locations: Slug Controlled Nebula, Slug Home Nebula, **`unique=false`** (matching the
  event's explicit attribute), `LRSmap=ship`.
- All three intro variants transcribed verbatim, matching `text_SLUG_REBEL_1` … `_3`.
- Outcome given only as "Fight a Rebel ship (default rewards)" — **no surrender or escape
  figures**. Those come from the `REBEL` ship block (50% surrender at `min=2 max=3`, 50%
  escape at `min=3 max=4`).
- Categorised under Fights with Default Rewards only.
