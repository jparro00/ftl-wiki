# Fandom — "Guides and tips"

## Summary
A link directory to community guides, tier lists and reverse-engineered data, retrieved at
revision 74605. Fetched because its **Sector guides** section is the only place the Fandom
wiki addresses "when to route here" at all — and it does so purely by pointing elsewhere.

## Key Takeaways
- **The sector-routing guidance the Fandom wiki has amounts to three outbound links** and no
  content of its own:
  - Crow Revell, *Sector guide* (2019), Google Doc;
  - Crow Revell, *Sector tier list* (2022), YouTube;
  - mekloz, *Sector profit data from 200× sector 4 hard*, Reddit — the same dataset cited by
    [[source-fandom-scrap]] and by [[source-fandom-sectors]]'s "colour is misleading" claim.
- Also links reverse-engineered game data (xftl on GitLab), Mike Hopley's scrap tables and
  weapon-timing calculators, and ship/weapon tier lists by named top players.
- **This repo holds none of those artefacts.** Any sector difficulty ranking or "route here
  when" advice added to `wiki/sectors/` would have to come from fetching them separately, and
  would be opinion by named players rather than data.
