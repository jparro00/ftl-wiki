# Fandom — "Pirate ship attacking civilian (Lanius)"

## Summary
The community wiki page for `LANIUS_PIRATE_CIVILIAN` — the Abandoned Sector's re-skin of
the standard pirate-attacking-civilian encounter. Retrieved at revision 73772.

## Key Takeaways
- **Names the in-game id:** *"This event is called 'LANIUS_PIRATE_CIVILIAN' in the
  datafiles."* (in a `Trivia` section)
- Locations: Abandoned Sector, `LRSmap=ship`, `unique=false`.
- Transcribes all three intro variants, all Lanius-flavoured.
- Enemy is a pirate ship (`PIRATE_CIVILIAN_LANIUS`); destroyed → medium, dead crew → high
  scrap-with-resources, then the shared "Contact the civilian ship" list.
- Categories: `Advanced Edition Content Events`, `Pirate ship fights`.
