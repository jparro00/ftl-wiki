# Fandom — "Slug fight in nebula"

## Summary
Community wiki page for `NEBULA_SLUG_FIGHT`, retrieved at revision 73870. Its value is the
`JELLY` surrender/escape figures and the explicit warning that this event is
indistinguishable from the Slug Cruiser unlock event.

## Key Takeaways
- Names the in-game id in Trivia: *"This event is called 'NEBULA_SLUG_FIGHT' in the
  datafiles."*
- Renders the ship's `min="3" max="4"` bands as **"50% surrender chance at 30–40% hull, 50%
  escape chance at 30–40% hull"** — the only source here that translates those attributes
  into a hull percentage.
- States that this event *"has identical intro and surrender texts to the Slug Home Nebula
  Surrender random event, but accepting the surrender yields entirely different results."*
- All five text variants match `text_events.xml` verbatim.
- `unique=false`, matching the files.
