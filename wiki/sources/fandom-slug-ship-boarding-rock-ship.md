## Summary
Community wiki page for `SLUG_DISTRESS_ROCK`, retrieved at revision 74822. Maps both
choices onto their outcome lists, including the post-fight `SLUG_DISTRESS_ROCK_RESULT`
roll.

## Key Takeaways
- Names the in-game id in Trivia: *"This event is called 'SLUG_DISTRESS_ROCK' in the
  datafiles."*
- Confirms the two-entry fight list (Slugs back down / Slugs engage) and the three-entry
  avoid list, marking the two identical "leave them alone" entries with `DuplicateEvent|2`.
- Confirms the stacked reward: `MED standard` for the Slug kill, plus a further
  `MED standard` if the freighter turns out to be abandoned.
- Confirms the Rock ship ambush on the ignore branch, at default rewards.
- Notes the event *"occurs at a regular distress beacon, but when you arrive there will be
  a nebula environment"* — matching the `<distressBeacon/>` + `<environment type="nebula"/>`
  pair in the files.
- Categories: `Nebula Events`, `Fights with Default Rewards`.
