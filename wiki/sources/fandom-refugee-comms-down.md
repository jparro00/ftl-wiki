# Fandom — "Refugee comms down"

## Summary
Full outcome list for the derelict refugee ship whose comms are dead. Declares the
datafile id: **"This event is called `REFUGEE_GHOST` in the datafiles."**

## Key Takeaways
- Five boarding outcomes: nothing, medium missiles reward, +1 crew, −1 crew
  (Clone Bay revives), or 2–4 human boarders.
- Interprets the medium `missiles` reward as **2–4 missiles** plus scrap.
- Confirms the Clone Bay text is a *revival*, not a failed revival.
- Sector scope: Civilian Sector, Pirate Controlled Sector, Uncharted Nebula; distress
  beacon; `unique=false`.
