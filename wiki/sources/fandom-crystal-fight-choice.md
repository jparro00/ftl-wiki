# Fandom — "Crystal fight choice"

## Summary
The community wiki page for `CRYSTAL_REBEL_CRYSTAL2`. Retrieved via the MediaWiki API at
revision 74026. A short page for a short event: help and get ambushed, or leave.

## Key Takeaways
- Names the in-game id: *"This event is called 'CRYSTAL_REBEL_CRYSTAL2' in the datafiles"*.
- Confirms there is **no** branch in which you fight the Rebel and **no** `CRYSTAL_SAVED`
  follow-up — the only fight available is against the Crystal ship that just won.
- `CRYSTAL_SHIP_NO_SURRENDER`: no surrender, no escape; default rewards.
- Location: Hidden Crystal Worlds, `unique=true`, **no ship** on Long-Range Scanners.
- Its title on the wiki ("Crystal fight choice") is easily confused with
  [[source-fandom-crystal-fight]] (`CRYSTAL_FIGHT`) and
  [[source-fandom-rebel-ship-attacking-crystal-ship]] (`CRYSTAL_REBEL_CRYSTAL`); the
  datafile ids are the reliable discriminator.
