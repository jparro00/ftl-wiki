# Fandom — "Crystal chat"

## Summary
The community wiki page for `CRYSTAL_CHATTY`. Retrieved via the MediaWiki API at revision
74025. Maps the nested conversation branch and its three-outcome payoff list.

## Key Takeaways
- Names the in-game id: *"This event is called 'CRYSTAL_CHATTY' in the datafiles"*.
- Quantifies `damage amount="-6"` as **6 repairs** and the blue-option reward as *"a random
  amount of fuel and scrap"*.
- Carries the batch's most detailed transcription of the shared **`REBEL` ship** entry —
  its `SurrenderEscape` footnote gives 50% surrender at 30–40% hull (3–4 threshold) and a
  50% escape at 20–30% hull. That data belongs to the ship, not this event, and is the
  reference used on [[event-rebel-fight-crystal]].
- Documents the Crystal-crew blue option (`req="crystal"`).
- Location: Hidden Crystal Worlds, `unique=true`, **no ship** on Long-Range Scanners.
