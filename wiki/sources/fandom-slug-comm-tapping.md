## Summary
Community wiki page for `QUEST_SLUG_PIRATE_TRAP`, retrieved at revision 73867. Covers the
trigger event and the `QUEST_SLUG_PIRATE_TRAP2` quest marker in one page, with every reward
tier attributed.

## Key Takeaways
- Names the in-game id in Trivia: *"This event is called 'QUEST_SLUG_PIRATE_TRAP' in the
  datafiles."*
- Notes that the quest marker sits at a **regular** beacon but arrives with a nebula
  environment — matching the `<environment type="nebula"/>` on `QUEST_SLUG_PIRATE_TRAP2`.
- All reward tiers match [[source-events-ships]]: `HIGH scrap_only` for taking the Slugs'
  split, `MED`/`HIGH standard` for killing `QUEST_SLUG_PIRATE_TRAP1`, `LOW`/`MED standard`
  for `QUEST_SLUG_PIRATE_TRAP2`.
- Describes `QUEST_SLUG_PIRATE_TRAP1`'s `chance="0"` surrender as simply *"surrenders at
  30–40% hull"* — the same reading it gives `JELLY_UNLOCK1` on
  [[source-fandom-slug-home-nebula-surrender]].
