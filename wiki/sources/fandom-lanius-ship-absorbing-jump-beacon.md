# Fandom — "Lanius ship absorbing jump beacon"

## Summary
The community wiki page for `LANIUS_BEACON_EATER`. Retrieved at revision 74232. The most
detailed of the Lanius pages: it maps all five top-level choices, both nested trade menus,
and marks one branch's weighting explicitly.

## Key Takeaways
- **Names the in-game id:** *"This event is called 'LANIUS_BEACON_EATER' in the
  datafiles."*
- Locations: Abandoned Sector, `LRSmap=ship`, `unique=true`, categorised as a
  Trading Event.
- Uses a `{{DuplicateEvent|3}}` marker on the "prepare to jump" outcome of the *Leave*
  branch — Fandom's own notation for an entry repeated three times in a four-member list,
  independently confirming the weighting visible in `LANIUS_BEACON_EATER_LEAVE`.
- Documents both blue options: **Lanius crew** (trade 30 scrap *or* 6 missiles *or* 6
  drone parts for a random augment, with no chance of a fight — the same 30-scrap price as
  the non-blue route, but with alternative currencies and no risk) and **Hull Repair
  Drone** (send the drone → gain a Lanius crew member).
- Warns that scrap spent on the "Send them 30 scrap" branch is **not refunded** if the
  Lanius attack anyway.
- Categories: `Advanced Edition Content Events`, `Fights with Default Rewards (Lanius)`,
  `Augmentation reward opportunity`, `Crew reward opportunity`, `Scrap loss risk`.
