# Fandom — "Rock and Slug standoff"

## Summary
Full outcome tree for the Rock/Slug reactor-payment dispute, including the shared "Slug
reward" sub-list. Declares the datafile id: **"This event is called `ROCK_SLUG_ARGUMENT`
in the datafiles."** Notes it is also reachable as
[[event-slug-and-rock-standoff-in-nebula]] in Slug sectors.

## Key Takeaways
- Paying off the debt costs 10–15 scrap; the optional reactor upgrade later costs another
  10–15 scrap.
- "Demand the Rock ship pay" has three results: they agree, they attack (Rock ship fight,
  low/medium standard rewards), or their reactor explodes.
- Sector scope: Rock Controlled Sector and Rock Homeworlds; `unique=true`.
