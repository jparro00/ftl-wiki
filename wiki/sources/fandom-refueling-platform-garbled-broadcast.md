# Fandom — "Refueling platform garbled broadcast"

## Summary
The community wiki page for `LANIUS_FUELING_STATION`. Retrieved at revision 73824. Maps
the full three-level choice tree, the three-member docking outcome list, and the
post-fight fuel payout.

## Key Takeaways
- **Names the in-game id:** *"This event is called 'LANIUS_FUELING_STATION' in the
  datafiles."* (in a `Trivia` section rather than `Notes`)
- Locations: Abandoned Sector, `LRSmap=noship`, `unique=true`.
- Quantifies every `item_modify`: 3-5 fuel from the safe docking result, +1-3 fuel for
  Sensors 2, +2-3 fuel and +1-3 drone parts for Sensors 3, a flat 5 fuel for the
  Blast Doors blue option, and 3-5 fuel from the post-victory platform sweep.
- Spells out the two ambush results: 3 hull + 3 engine damage, or a hull breach plus one
  Lanius boarder — both alongside a `LANIUS_FUELING_STATION_SHIP` fight.
- Categories: `Advanced Edition Content Events`, `Hull damage risk`, `System damage risk`,
  `Hull breach risk`, `Boarding risk`, `Fuel reward opportunity`,
  `Drone Parts reward chance`.
