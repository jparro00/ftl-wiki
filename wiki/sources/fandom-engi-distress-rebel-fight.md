# Fandom — Engi distress Rebel fight

## Summary
FTL Fandom wiki page for `DISTRESS_ENGI_REBEL` (revision 74045, retrieved 2026-08-09).
Covers both the forced Rebel fight and the post-combat trading event
`DISTRESS_ENGI_REBEL_RESULT`, which it presents as an "After the fight" section of the
same page.

## Key Takeaways
- Names the three possible payouts of the 25-scrap option: nothing, Healing Burst, or a
  drone schematic — matching the three entries of `DISTRESS_ENGI_REBEL_LIST1`.
- Names the 40-scrap/2-missile/2-fuel payout as the **Engi Med-bot Dispersal**
  augmentation (`NANO_MEDBAY` in the files).
- Confirms destroyed → low scrap-with-resources, dead crew → medium.
