# Fandom — "The Rebellion"

## Key Takeaways
- The Rebellion is a human-supremacist movement that has all but defeated the Federation.
  No alien ever serves on a Rebel ship; some Rebels spare all-human crews.
- Lists the Rebel ship types encountered: Fighter, Invader, Elite Fighter, Elite Assault,
  Rigger, Disruptor, Auto-Scout, Auto-Surveyor, Auto-Assault, Auto-Hacker, the Flagship, and
  two Cruiser classes that exist **only as unused images** (`fleet_1_med_warning`,
  `fleet_2_med_warning`).
- Notes the Rebel–Engi non-aggression pact, broken covertly by both sides.
