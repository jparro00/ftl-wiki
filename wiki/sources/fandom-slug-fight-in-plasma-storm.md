# Fandom — "Slug fight in plasma storm"

## Summary
Community wiki page for `STORM_SLUG_FIGHT`, retrieved at revision 73877. Its distinctive
contribution is a consequence the game files only imply: because this storm beacon carries
**no nebula tag**, jumping out of it costs full Rebel fleet pursuit rather than the reduced
nebula rate.

## Key Takeaways
- Names the in-game id in Trivia: *"This event is called 'STORM_SLUG_FIGHT' in the
  datafiles."*
- Locations: Slug Controlled Nebula, Slug Home Nebula, `unique=false`,
  `LRSmap=ship+plasmastorm`.
- Opens with an explicit note: *"This event has a plasma storm environment, but the beacon
  is not inside a nebula."* — matching `<environment type="storm"/>` with no nebula.
- **Fleet-pursuit claim:** *"Fleet pursuit will be the full amount, instead of the 80% that
  you would have when jumping from a nebula beacon in a Slug sector."* The 80% figure
  appears nowhere in the game files and is Fandom's own.
- Renders the `JELLY` ship as "50% surrender offer chance at 30–40% hull, 50% escape
  attempt chance at 30–40% hull", agreeing with [[concept-surrender-offers]]'s reading of
  `chance="0.5"`.
- All four intro variants transcribed verbatim.
