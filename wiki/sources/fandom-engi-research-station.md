# Fandom — Engi research station

## Summary
FTL Fandom wiki page for the `DISTRESS_ENGI_REACTOR` distress beacon (revision 74048,
retrieved 2026-08-09). Transcribes the full branch tree including the two Clone Bay
sub-branches that the raw event XML expresses only as `<removeCrew><clone>true</clone>`.

## Key Takeaways
- Confirms the event is unique and appears at distress beacons in Engi Controlled Sector
  and Engi Homeworlds.
- Documents the "Board the station" list as a 50/50-looking split between a dead station
  (nothing) and the reactor-overload branch — **no odds are given**.
- States explicitly that the Engi crewmember is awarded *regardless* of Clone Bay
  availability on the "Save the Engi!" branch — a reading of the XML that is otherwise
  easy to get wrong.
- Adds a third sub-choice, "Save yourselves!", present in the XML but absent from the
  batch extract.
