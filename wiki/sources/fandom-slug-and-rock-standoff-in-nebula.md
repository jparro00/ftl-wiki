# Fandom — "Slug and Rock standoff in nebula"

## Summary
Short wrapper page: the nebula version of the Rock/Slug standoff. "Get closer" loads the
[[event-rock-and-slug-standoff]] event directly, with the nebula environment preserved.
Declares the datafile id: **"This event is called `ROCK_SLUG_ARGUMENT_NEBULA` in the
datafiles."**

## Key Takeaways
- Two choices only: get closer (loads the standoff) or ignore (nothing).
- Sector scope: Slug Controlled Nebula and Slug Home Nebula; nebula environment;
  `unique=true`.
- Explicitly notes the nebula environment persists into the loaded event.
