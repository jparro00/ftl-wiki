# Fandom — The Engi virus

## Summary
FTL Fandom wiki page for `ENGI_VIRUS` (revision 74725, retrieved 2026-08-09). A DLC unique
event with the densest blue-option tree in Engi space: five distinct gates (Hacking 1/2/3,
Engi crew, Lanius crew), each with a different payout.

## Key Takeaways
- Names the augment awarded by four of the five blue options as **Drone Reactor Booster**
  (`DRONE_SPEED` in the files).
- Warns that taking the Engi-crew option when the Engi is your *only* crewmember is a
  game over.
- States that the Clone Bay explicitly cannot revive the Engi killed by that option —
  matching `<clone>false</clone>` in the files.
- Documents the reborn crewmember "Virus" as maxed in all skills, matching
  `all_skills="2"`.
- Repeats the `MEDIUM` vs `MED` `autoReward` note in three places, claiming the engine
  falls back to `RANDOM`.
