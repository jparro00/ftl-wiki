# Fandom — "Lanius with Federation science craft"

## Summary
Community wiki page for `LANIUS_RESEARCHER_CONTACT`, retrieved via the MediaWiki API at
revision 74246. Lays out both reward tables — the ordinary four-way roll and the Lanius
blue option's three-way item roll — and confirms the event has no fight and no cost.

## Key Takeaways
- **Names the in-game id**: *"This event is called 'LANIUS_RESEARCHER_CONTACT' in the
  datafiles."*
- Location: Abandoned Sector; `LRSmap=noship`, `unique=true`.
- Lists all four `LANIUS_RESEARCHER_CONTACT_LIST` outcomes (weapon / drone parts / medium
  scrap / nothing) and the blue option's weapon-or-drone-or-augment result — matching the
  XML exactly in content, though in a different order.
- Categorised as *Advanced Edition Content Events*, plus reward-chance categories for
  augmentation, drone schematics, weapons and drone parts.
- States no odds for either list.
