# Fandom — "Slug hacker (oxygen)"

## Summary
Community wiki page for `NEBULA_SLUG_OXYGEN`, retrieved at revision 74294. Three choices,
all leading to the same fight with different degrees of life support. Its intro text is
visibly older than the game files'.

## Key Takeaways
- Names the in-game id in Trivia: *"This event is called 'NEBULA_SLUG_OXYGEN' in the
  datafiles."*
- Confirms the enemy carries a Fire Beam or Fire Bomb, and that it **does not surrender or
  escape** — matching `JELLY_STATUS_OXYGEN_FIRE` in [[source-events-ships]].
- Reward is `medium` scrap-with-resources regardless of the choice — matching the ship's
  `MED standard` on both `destroyed` and `deadCrew`.
- Claims this is *"the only event which contains a blue option for (upgraded) Oxygen
  system"* — and then, in the next bullet, says the upgraded Oxygen system "can be similarly
  used in the Dangerous-looking ship event". The two bullets sit awkwardly together; treat
  the "only event" claim as unreliable.
