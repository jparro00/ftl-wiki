# Fandom — "Rebel checkpoint"

## Summary
Lists the four intro variants, the three choices, the bribe cost, and the four possible
results of contacting the civilians afterwards. Declares the datafile id:
**"This event is called `REBEL_CHECKPOINT` in the datafiles."**

## Key Takeaways
- Bribe costs 10–15 scrap.
- The follow-up "Contact the civilian ships" branch has four outcomes: nothing, low
  scrap-only, low standard reward, or a Rebel ship fight.
- Hiding always does nothing.
- Marked a filler / exit event for the Slug sectors.
