# Fandom — "Sensors"

## Summary
The Sensors subsystem page, retrieved at revision 73457. Fetched for one reason: it states
exactly what a nebula costs you, which is the missing half of the nebula trade-off that
governs whether a nebula sector is worth routing into.

## Key Takeaways
- Level 1 shows your own ship's interior; 2 adds the enemy interior; 3 adds enemy weapon
  charge bars; 4 adds enemy system levels, power usage, ion damage/cooldown and
  repair/sabotage progress. Level 4 is reachable **only by manning** a level-3 system.
- **Sensors are temporarily disabled in nebulas** (and by the jammed-sensors boarding event)
  — so the fleet-slowing benefit of a nebula beacon is paid for with total loss of enemy
  interior information, unless you carry Slug crew or a Lifeform Scanner
  ([[source-fandom-environmental-hazards]]).
- Level 2+ Sensors reveal enemy crew name and HP **even through enemy cloaking**.
- Enemy ships have no Sensors subsystem but always have full information about you.
- The Rebel Flagship caps your Sensors at level 2.
- Upgrade costs: 40 / 25 / 40 scrap for levels 1–3; level 4 is not purchasable.
- Upgraded Sensors gate some blue options.
