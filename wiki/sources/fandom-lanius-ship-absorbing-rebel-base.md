# Fandom — "Lanius ship absorbing rebel base"

## Summary
The community wiki page for `LANIUS_GROUP_AUTO`. Retrieved at revision 74233. Documents
all three `LANIUS_GROUP_AUTO_LIST` outcomes and the Lanius-crew blue option that skips
the gamble entirely.

## Key Takeaways
- **Names the in-game id:** *"This event is called 'LANIUS_GROUP_AUTO' in the datafiles."*
- Locations: Abandoned Sector, `LRSmap=noship`, `unique=true`.
- The gamble's three results: medium scrap + 1-turn Rebel fleet delay; a `LANIUS_SHIP`
  fight; or nothing.
- The blue option gives the good result outright: medium scrap-with-resources **and** the
  1-turn fleet delay.
- Categories: `Advanced Edition Content Events`, `Fights with Default Rewards (Lanius)`,
  `Rebel Fleet delay opportunity`.
