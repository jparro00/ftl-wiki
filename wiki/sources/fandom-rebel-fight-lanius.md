## Key Takeaways
- **Names the in-game id:** *"This event is called 'LANIUS_REBEL_FIGHT' in the
  datafiles."* (in a `Trivia` section)
- Locations: Abandoned Sector, `LRSmap=ship`, `unique=false`.

  of how the Rebellion is reacting to the Lanius incursion.

  definition.
