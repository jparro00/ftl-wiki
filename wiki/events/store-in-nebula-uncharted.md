# Store in nebula (Uncharted) — `NEBULA_STORE`

## Summary
The guaranteed store beacon of [[sector-uncharted-nebula]]. Five flavour texts,
`<environment type="nebula"/>`, and a bare `<store/>` element. `sector_data.xml` allocates
it `min=1 max=1`, so every Uncharted Nebula has exactly one of these — on top of the
sector's separate `STORE` allocation of 0–1.

## Trigger & Where It Appears
- Beacon: **store**, inside a nebula.
- **Directly allocated**, not pooled: `sector_data.xml` gives `NEBULA_SECTOR` the line
  `<event name="NEBULA_STORE" min="1" max="1"/>` ([[source-sector-data-xml]]). It appears
  in **no event list at all** — that is why the batch shows `lists: []`.
- No `unique` attribute, but the `min=1 max=1` allocation makes it effectively once per
  sector.
- Long-range scanners show no ship ([[source-fandom-store-in-nebula-uncharted]]).
- The same sector separately allocates `STORE` at `min=0 max=1`, so an Uncharted Nebula can
  hold **one or two** stores in total ([[source-sector-data-xml]]).

## Text
The prose is drawn from the `NEBULA_STORE` text list and **varies across five strings**
([[source-events-nebula]], [[source-text-events-xml]]). All five are transcribed on
[[source-fandom-store-in-nebula-uncharted]]:

> Some brave entrepreneurs set up a station here to extract elements from the nebula. There
> seems to be a public shipyard and rest stop here as well.

> The centre of a hydrogen cloud doesn't seem like a very usual place for a store, but the
> Engi aren't a usual species. Wouldn't hurt to take a look.

> You discover a cluster of Federation refugee ships taking shelter in the folds of the
> nebula. "I'm sorry we've got nothing to give you, but we need scrap to keep the engines
> running." You sense they'll strike a trade.

Note the text list is also named `NEBULA_STORE` — a `textList` sharing an event's name,
which unlike the `eventList` collisions on [[event-pirate-fight-in-nebula]] and
[[event-rebel-fight-in-nebula]] causes no problem.

## Choices & Outcomes

| # | Choice | Requirement | Outcome(s) | Odds |
|---|--------|-------------|-----------|------|
| — | _(none — the event has no `<choice>` elements)_ | — | A store opens. | 100% |

The complete event body is `<text load="NEBULA_STORE"/>`,
`<environment type="nebula"/>` and `<store/>` ([[source-events-nebula]]). The store's stock
is generated by the game rather than specified here — nothing in this event constrains what
is for sale.

## Blue Options
None.

## Rewards & Risks
Neither, beyond the store's own prices. The beacon is safe.

## Strategy Notes
- This is the strongest single argument for routing into [[sector-uncharted-nebula]]: a
  **guaranteed** store, plus a 0–1 chance of a second one, in a sector that otherwise
  offers thin rewards.
- The store sits in a nebula, so jumping *out* of it carries whatever fleet-pursuit
  discount nebula beacons confer — see the note on
  [[source-fandom-rebel-fight-in-plasma-storm]].
- The Slug sectors have their own equivalent, `NEBULA_STORE_SLUG`, allocated `min=2 max=2`
  — two guaranteed stores — which is a separate event with its own page
  ([[source-events-slug]], [[source-sector-data-xml]]).
