# Store (Mantis) — `STORE_MANTIS`

## Summary
The Mantis-flavoured store beacon. Six flavour intros wrapped around a single `<store/>`
tag — mechanically identical to every other faction store, with the flavour explaining why
Mantis are selling to you at all rather than eating you.

## Trigger & Where It Appears
- Sectors: [[sector-mantis-controlled-sector]], [[sector-mantis-homeworlds]]
- Allocated directly by name in both Mantis sector descriptions at `min=1 max=2`
  ([[source-sector-data-xml]], per `raw/gamedata/sector_data.xml`) — so every Mantis
  sector has one or two stores.
- No `unique` attribute.
- Marked `store=true` on Fandom; long-range scanners show no ship
  ([[source-fandom-store-mantis]]).

## Text
Drawn from the `STORE_MANTIS` text list — the prose **varies** across six strings
([[source-events-xml]], [[source-text-events-xml]]):

> Upon arriving at the beacon, you're hailed by a well-spoken Mantis, "Hail, traveler.
> These are dangerous times. Perhaps you find yourself in need of our services?" He
> appears to be a trader.

> Uncertain about what you'll discover at this beacon, you scan the surroundings. You
> detect several warnings on wide-band channels discouraging any violence in protected
> trade-space. Perhaps you can find some wares nearby.

> Merchants are not highly respected among the Mantis race, so few undertake the
> profession. You're mildly surprised to receive an advertisement from a nearby space
> dock. You decide to see what they have to offer.

> Identifying a Mantis trading post ahead, you signal them so that your approach won't be
> taken as a threat. The leader hails you: "You have scrap? Quickly, come dock before the
> warriors see you."

> A struggling Mantis cargo ship headed for the node raises what's left of shields as you
> jump in. You power up your weapons as a show of force, and they reluctantly agree to
> offload some of their cargo to you - for a price.

> The Rebels are already here! The crew braces for impact, but none comes. The sensors
> report the unmanned rebel supply ship is malfunctioning and is stuck in vending mode -
> for a little scrap you can take your pick.

## Blue Options
None.

## Rewards & Risks
- Neither, beyond the store itself. The event carries no `autoReward` or hazard.
- **The flavour text does not change the stock.** All six variants — including the
  "malfunctioning Rebel supply ship" one — resolve to the same bare `<store/>` element
  ([[source-events-xml]]). The store's contents are generated by the game's store logic,
  not by this event.
- Mantis sectors set a `rarityList` that biases *ship and crew* blueprint frequency
  (`mantis` rarity 1, `human` 2, `engi` 3, `rock` 4, `energy`/`slug` 0)
  ([[source-sector-data-xml]]). Whether that list also affects store stock is **not**
  stated in the sources read here.

## Strategy Notes
- Mantis sectors guarantee 1–2 stores, the same allocation as most faction sectors
  ([[source-sector-data-xml]]) — so routing into Mantis space does not cost you shopping
  opportunities, only hull.
- *(Opinion.)* Given the sector's 6–7 hostile beacons plus 1–2 boarding beacons, the
  Mantis-sector store is the natural place to buy door upgrades or anti-boarding tools
  before the rest of the sector happens to you.
